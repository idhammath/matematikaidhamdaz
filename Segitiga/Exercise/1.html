Misal \(CG = x\)
\[OG = \dfrac{3}{2}\sqrt{26} - x\] \[\begin{aligned} OF^2 - OG^2 &= CF^2 - CG^2 \\ \left(\dfrac{9}{2}\sqrt{2}\right)^2 - \left(\dfrac{3}{2}\sqrt{26} - x\right)^2 &= \left(6\sqrt{2}\right)^2 - x^2 \\ \dfrac{81}{2} - \left(\dfrac{117}{2} - 3x\sqrt{26} + x^2\right) &= 72 - x^2 \\ \dfrac{81}{2} - \dfrac{117}{2} + 3x\sqrt{26} - x^2 &= 72 - x^2 \\ -18 + 3x\sqrt{26} &= 72 \\ 3x\sqrt{26} &= 90 \\ x &= \dfrac{90}{3\sqrt{26}} \\ &= \dfrac{30}{\sqrt{26}} \end{aligned}\] \[\begin{aligned} FG &= \sqrt{CF^2 - CG^2} \\ &= \sqrt{\left(6\sqrt{2}\right)^2 - \left(\dfrac{30}{\sqrt{26}}\right)^2} \\ &= \sqrt{72 - \dfrac{900}{26}} \\ &= \sqrt{72 - \dfrac{450}{13}} \\ &= \sqrt{\dfrac{486}{13}} \dots \text{ (dari } 72 - \dfrac{900}{13} = \dfrac{36}{13}\text{)} \\ &= \sqrt{\dfrac{36}{13}} \\ &= \dfrac{6}{13}\sqrt{13} \end{aligned}\]
Jadi, \(FG = \dfrac{6}{13}\sqrt{13}\text{ cm}\).
