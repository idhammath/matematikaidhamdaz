\[\lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{x}{2\csc x\left(1-\sqrt{\cos x}\right)}=\dots\]

  1. \(-2\)
  2. \(-1\)
  3. \(0\)
  4. \(1\)
  5. \(2\)
Grup WhatsApp
Pembahasan:

Ubah \(\csc x = \dfrac{1}{\sin x}\), lalu kalikan sekawan akar dan kosinus berturut-turut:

\[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{x}{2\csc x\left(1-\sqrt{\cos x}\right)} &= \lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{x\sin x}{2\left(1-\sqrt{\cos x}\right)}\cdot\dfrac{1+\sqrt{\cos x}}{1+\sqrt{\cos x}}\\[3.7pt] &= \lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{x\sin x\left(1+\sqrt{\cos x}\right)}{2(1-\cos x)}\cdot\dfrac{1+\cos x}{1+\cos x}\\[3.7pt] &= \lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{x\sin x\left(1+\sqrt{\cos x}\right)(1+\cos x)}{2(1-\cos^2 x)}\\[3.7pt] &= \lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{x\sin x\left(1+\sqrt{\cos x}\right)(1+\cos x)}{2\sin^2 x}\\[3.7pt] &= \lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{x}{\sin x}\cdot\lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{\left(1+\sqrt{\cos x}\right)(1+\cos x)}{2}\\[3.7pt] &= 1\cdot\dfrac{(1+\sqrt{1})(1+1)}{2}\\[3.7pt] &= \dfrac{2(2)}{2} = 2 \end{aligned} \]
Jadi, hasilnya adalah \(2\).

  1. \(0\)
  2. \(-\dfrac{1}{9}\)
  3. \(\dfrac{1}{2}\)
  4. \(2\)
  5. \(4\)
Grup WhatsApp
Pembahasan:

Faktorkan pembilang dan penyebut:

\[ \begin{aligned} x^2-5x+6 &= (x-3)(x-2)\\[3.7pt] x^2-x-2 &= (x+1)(x-2) \implies (x^2-x-2)^2 = (x+1)^2(x-2)^2 \end{aligned} \] Substitusikan ke dalam bentuk limit: \[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{x\to2}\dfrac{(x-3)(x-2)\sin(x-2)}{(x+1)^2(x-2)^2} &= \lim_{x\to2}\left[\dfrac{x-3}{(x+1)^2}\cdot\dfrac{\sin(x-2)}{x-2}\right]\\[3.7pt] &= \dfrac{2-3}{(2+1)^2}\cdot 1\\[3.7pt] &= \dfrac{-1}{3^2} = -\dfrac{1}{9} \end{aligned} \]
Jadi, hasilnya adalah \(-\dfrac{1}{9}\).

Nilai dari: \[\lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{\sin 3x+\sin 5x}{2x\cos 4x}=\dots\]

Brainly.co.id
Pembahasan:

Bagi pembilang dan penyebut dengan \(x\):

\[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{\sin 3x+\sin 5x}{2x\cos 4x} &= \lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{\dfrac{\sin 3x}{x}+\dfrac{\sin 5x}{x}}{2\cos 4x}\\[3.7pt] &= \dfrac{3 + 5}{2\cos(0)}\\[3.7pt] &= \dfrac{8}{2(1)} = 4 \end{aligned} \]
Jadi, \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{\sin 3x+\sin 5x}{2x\cos 4x}=4\).

  2. \(-1\)
  3. \(-2\)
  4. \(1\)
  5. \(2\)
Grup WhatsApp
Pembahasan:

Ubah identitas kosinus sudut ganda \(\cos 2x = 1 - 2\sin^2 x\):

\[ \begin{aligned} \sin^2 x - \cos 2x + 1 &= \sin^2 x - (1 - 2\sin^2 x) + 1\\[3.7pt] &= \sin^2 x - 1 + 2\sin^2 x + 1 = 3\sin^2 x \end{aligned} \] Substitusikan ke bentuk limit: \[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{x\tan 3x}{3\sin^2 x} &= \lim_{x\to0}\left(\dfrac{1}{3}\cdot\dfrac{x}{\sin x}\cdot\dfrac{\tan 3x}{\sin x}\right)\\[3.7pt] &= \dfrac{1}{3}\cdot 1 \cdot 3 = 1 \end{aligned} \]
Jadi, hasilnya adalah \(1\).

\[\lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{4\tan 5x}{3x}=\dots\]

Grup Telegram
Pembahasan:

Berdasarkan teorema limit fungsi trigonometri dasar \(\lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{\tan ax}{x} = a\):

\[ \lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{4\tan 5x}{3x} = \dfrac{4}{3}\cdot\lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{\tan 5x}{x} = \dfrac{4}{3}\cdot 5 = \dfrac{20}{3} \]
Jadi, \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{4\tan 5x}{3x}=\dfrac{20}{3}\).

Tentukan nilai dari: \[\lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{2-2\cos 2x}{x^2}\]

Grup Telegram
Pembahasan:

Gunakan rumus sudut rangkap \(\cos 2x = 1 - 2\sin^2 x\):

\[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{2-2\cos 2x}{x^2} &= \lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{2 - 2(1 - 2\sin^2 x)}{x^2}\\[3.7pt] &= \lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{4\sin^2 x}{x^2}\\[3.7pt] &= 4\cdot\lim_{x\to0}\left(\dfrac{\sin x}{x}\right)^2\\[3.7pt] &= 4(1)^2 = 4 \end{aligned} \]
Jadi, \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{2-2\cos 2x}{x^2}=4\).

Nilai dari: \[\lim_{x\to4}\dfrac{x^2-16}{\sin(2x-8)}=\dots\]

Grup Telegram
Pembahasan:

Faktorkan pembilang \(x^2 - 16 = (x + 4)(x - 4)\) dan penyebut \(\sin(2x - 8) = \sin 2(x - 4)\):

\[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{x\to4}\dfrac{x^2-16}{\sin(2x-8)} &= \lim_{x\to4}\left[(x+4)\cdot\dfrac{x-4}{\sin 2(x-4)}\right] \end{aligned} \] Misalkan \(t = x - 4\), saat \(x \to 4\) maka \(t \to 0\): \[ \begin{aligned} &= (4 + 4)\cdot\lim_{t\to0}\dfrac{t}{\sin 2t}\\[3.7pt] &= 8\cdot\dfrac{1}{2} = 4 \end{aligned} \]
Jadi, \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to4}\dfrac{x^2-16}{\sin(2x-8)}=4\).

\[\lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{1-\cos 4x}{x\sin x}=\dots\]

Grup Telegram
Pembahasan:

Gunakan identitas sudut rangkap \(1 - \cos 4x = 2\sin^2 2x\):

\[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{1-\cos 4x}{x\sin x} &= \lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{2\sin^2 2x}{x\sin x}\\[3.7pt] &= 2\cdot\lim_{x\to0}\left(\dfrac{\sin 2x}{x}\cdot\dfrac{\sin 2x}{\sin x}\right)\\[3.7pt] &= 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 = 8 \end{aligned} \]
Jadi, \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{1-\cos 4x}{x\sin x}=8\).
