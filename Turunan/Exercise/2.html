Apabila $f(x)=x^2-\dfrac{1}{x}+1$, maka $f'(x)$ adalah ....

  1. $x - x^2$
  2. $x + x^2$
  3. $2x - x^2 + 1$
  4. $2x - x^2 - 1$
  5. $2x + x^2$
Grup Telegram
Pembahasan:
$$\begin{aligned} f(x)&=x^2-\dfrac{1}{x}+1\\ &=x^2-x^{-1}+1\\ f'(x)&=2x-(-1)x^{-2}\\ &=2x+\dfrac{1}{x^2} \end{aligned}$$
Jadi, $f'(x)=2x+\dfrac{1}{x^2}$. (JAWAB: TIDAK ADA)

Tentukan turunan dari fungsi berikut:

$$f(x) = x^{12}$$
Grup Telegram
Pembahasan:
$$\begin{aligned} f(x)&=x^{12}\\ f'(x)&=\boxed{\boxed{12x^{11}}} \end{aligned}$$
Jadi, turunan dari $f(x) = x^{12}$ adalah $f'(x) = 12x^{11}$.

Jika $f(t)=\dfrac{t}{t^2+1}$, nilai $\dfrac{df(t)}{dt}$ adalah ....

  1. $\dfrac{1}{2t}$
  2. $\dfrac{1-t^2}{2t}$
  3. $\dfrac{1-t^2}{\left(t^2+1\right)^2}$
  4. $\dfrac{3t^2+1}{\left(t^2+1\right)^2}$
  5. $2t^2\left(t^2 + 1\right)^{-2}$
Whatsapp Tanya Matematika
Pembahasan:
$$\begin{aligned} u&=t\\ u'&=1 \end{aligned} \qquad\qquad \begin{aligned} v&=t^2+1\\ v'&=2t \end{aligned}$$ $$\begin{aligned} \dfrac{df(t)}{dt}&=\dfrac{u'v-uv'}{v^2}\\[3.8pt] &=\dfrac{1\left(t^2+1\right)-t(2t)}{\left(t^2+1\right)^2}\\[3.8pt] &=\dfrac{t^2+1-2t^2}{\left(t^2+1\right)^2}\\ &=\boxed{\boxed{\dfrac{1-t^2}{\left(t^2+1\right)^2}}} \end{aligned}$$
Jadi, $\dfrac{df(t)}{dt}=\dfrac{1-t^2}{\left(t^2+1\right)^2}$. (JAWAB: C)

Dengan menggunakan konsep limit fungsi, tentukan gradien garis singgung fungsi berikut.

  1. $f(x) = 3x^2 - 2x + 1$
  2. $f(x) = x^3 - x$
  3. $f(x) = x^3 - x^{-3}$
  4. $f(x) = 2(1 - x)^2$
  5. $f(x)=\dfrac{2}{x}$
Buku Sekolah Digital Matematika Kelas 11 SMA K13
Pembahasan:

  1. $m=\displaystyle\lim_{\Delta x\to0}\dfrac{f(x+\Delta x)-f(x)}{\Delta x}$

    $$\begin{aligned} m&=\lim_{\Delta x\to0}\dfrac{3(x+\Delta x)^2-2(x+\Delta x)+1-\left(3x^2-2x+1\right)}{\Delta x}\\[3.8pt] &=\lim_{\Delta x\to0}\dfrac{3\left(x^2+2x\Delta x+\Delta x^2\right)-2x-2\Delta x+1-3x^2+2x-1}{\Delta x}\\[3.8pt] &=\lim_{\Delta x\to0}\dfrac{3x^2+6x\Delta x+3\Delta x^2-2\Delta x-3x^2}{\Delta x}\\[3.8pt] &=\lim_{\Delta x\to0}\dfrac{6x\Delta x+3\Delta x^2-2\Delta x}{\Delta x}\\[3.8pt] &=\lim_{\Delta x\to0}(6x+3\Delta x-2)\\ &=6x+3(0)-2\\ &=\boxed{\boxed{6x-2}} \end{aligned}$$

  2. $m=\displaystyle\lim_{\Delta x\to0}\dfrac{f(x+\Delta x)-f(x)}{\Delta x}$

    $$\begin{aligned} m&=\lim_{\Delta x\to0}\dfrac{(x+\Delta x)^3-(x+\Delta x)-\left(x^3-x\right)}{\Delta x}\\[3.8pt] &=\lim_{\Delta x\to0}\dfrac{x^3+3x^2\Delta x+3x\Delta x^2+\Delta x^3-x-\Delta x-x^3+x}{\Delta x}\\[3.8pt] &=\lim_{\Delta x\to0}\dfrac{3x^2\Delta x+3x\Delta x^2+\Delta x^3-\Delta x}{\Delta x}\\[3.8pt] &=\lim_{\Delta x\to0}\left(3x^2+3x\Delta x+\Delta x^2-1\right)\\ &=3x^2+3x(0)+(0)^2-1\\ &=\boxed{\boxed{3x^2-1}} \end{aligned}$$

  3. Cara 1

    $f(x)=x^3-x^{-3}=x^3-\dfrac{1}{x^3}=\dfrac{x^6-1}{x^3}$

    $$\begin{aligned} m&=\lim_{\Delta x\to0}\dfrac{f(x+\Delta x)-f(x)}{\Delta x}\\[3.8pt] &=\lim_{\Delta x\to0}\dfrac{\dfrac{(x+\Delta x)^6-1}{(x+\Delta x)^3}-\left(\dfrac{x^6-1}{x^3}\right)}{\Delta x}\\[3.8pt] &=\lim_{\Delta x\to0}\dfrac{\dfrac{x^3\left((x+\Delta x)^6-1\right)-(x+\Delta x)^3\left(x^6-1\right)}{(x+\Delta x)^3x^3}}{\Delta x}\\[3.8pt] &=\lim_{\Delta x\to0}\dfrac{x^3(x+\Delta x)^6-x^3-x^6(x+\Delta x)^3+(x+\Delta x)^3}{\Delta x(x+\Delta x)^3x^3}\\[3.8pt] &=\lim_{\Delta x\to0}\dfrac{x^3(x+\Delta x)^6-x^6(x+\Delta x)^3+(x+\Delta x)^3-x^3}{\Delta x(x+\Delta x)^3x^3}\\[3.8pt] &=\lim_{\Delta x\to0}\dfrac{x^3(x+\Delta x)^3\left((x+\Delta x)^3-x^3\right)+(x+\Delta x)^3-x^3}{\Delta x(x+\Delta x)^3x^3}\\[3.8pt] &=\lim_{\Delta x\to0}\dfrac{\left(x^3(x+\Delta x)^3+1\right)\left((x+\Delta x)^3-x^3\right)}{\Delta x(x+\Delta x)^3x^3}\\[3.8pt] &=\lim_{\Delta x\to0}\dfrac{\left(x^3(x+\Delta x)^3+1\right)\left(x^3+3x^2\Delta x+3x\Delta x^2+\Delta x^3-x^3\right)}{\Delta x(x+\Delta x)^3x^3}\\[3.8pt] &=\lim_{\Delta x\to0}\dfrac{\left(x^3(x+\Delta x)^3+1\right)\left(3x^2\Delta x+3x\Delta x^2+\Delta x^3\right)}{\Delta x(x+\Delta x)^3x^3}\\[3.8pt] &=\lim_{\Delta x\to0}\dfrac{\left(x^3(x+\Delta x)^3+1\right)\Delta x\left(3x^2+3x\Delta x+\Delta x^2\right)}{\Delta x(x+\Delta x)^3x^3}\\[3.8pt] &=\lim_{\Delta x\to0}\dfrac{\left(x^3(x+\Delta x)^3+1\right)\left(3x^2+3x\Delta x+\Delta x^2\right)}{(x+\Delta x)^3x^3}\\[3.8pt] &=\dfrac{\left(x^3(x+0)^3+1\right)\left(3x^2+3x(0)+(0)^2\right)}{(x+0)^3x^3}\\[3.8pt] &=\dfrac{\left(x^3(x)^3+1\right)\left(3x^2\right)}{(x)^3x^3}\\[3.8pt] &=\dfrac{\left(x^6+1\right)\left(3x^2\right)}{x^6}\\ &=\dfrac{3\left(x^6+1\right)}{x^4}\\ &=\dfrac{3x^6+3}{x^4}\\ &=\dfrac{3x^6}{x^4}+\dfrac{3}{x^4}\\ &=\boxed{\boxed{3x^2+3x^{-4}}} \end{aligned}$$

    Cara 2

    $$\begin{aligned} m&=\lim_{\Delta x\to0}\dfrac{f(x+\Delta x)-f(x)}{\Delta x}\\[3.8pt] &=\lim_{\Delta x\to0}\dfrac{(x+\Delta x)^3-\dfrac{1}{(x+\Delta x)^3}-x^3+\dfrac{1}{x^3}}{\Delta x}\\[3.8pt] &=\lim_{\Delta x\to0}\dfrac{(x+\Delta x)^3-x^3+\dfrac{1}{x^3}-\dfrac{1}{(x+\Delta x)^3}}{\Delta x}\\[3.8pt] &=\lim_{\Delta x\to0}\dfrac{x^3+3x^2\Delta x+3x\Delta x^2+\Delta x^3-x^3+\dfrac{(x+\Delta x)^3-x^3}{x^3(x+\Delta x)^3}}{\Delta x}\\[3.8pt] &=\lim_{\Delta x\to0}\dfrac{3x^2\Delta x+3x\Delta x^2+\Delta x^3+\dfrac{x^3+3x^2\Delta x+3x\Delta x^2+\Delta x^3-x^3}{x^3(x+\Delta x)^3}}{\Delta x}\\[3.8pt] &=\lim_{\Delta x\to0}\dfrac{3x^2\Delta x+3x\Delta x^2+\Delta x^3+\dfrac{3x^2\Delta x+3x\Delta x^2+\Delta x^3}{x^3(x+\Delta x)^3}}{\Delta x}\\[3.8pt] &=\lim_{\Delta x\to0}\left(3x^2+3x\Delta x+\Delta x^2+\dfrac{3x^2+3x\Delta x+\Delta x^2}{x^3(x+\Delta x)^3}\right)\\[3.8pt] &=3x^2+3x(0)+0^2+\dfrac{3x^2+3x(0)+0^2}{x^3(x+0)^3}\\[3.8pt] &=3x^2+\dfrac{3x^2}{x^6}\\ &=3x^2+\dfrac{3}{x^4}\\ &=\boxed{\boxed{3x^2+3x^{-4}}} \end{aligned}$$

  4. $m=\displaystyle\lim_{\Delta x\to0}\dfrac{f(x+\Delta x)-f(x)}{\Delta x}$

    $$\begin{aligned} m&=\lim_{\Delta x\to0}\dfrac{2\left(1-(x+\Delta x)\right)^2-2(1-x)^2}{\Delta x}\\[3.8pt] &=\lim_{\Delta x\to0}\dfrac{2\left(1-x-\Delta x\right)^2-2\left(1-2x+x^2\right)}{\Delta x}\\[3.8pt] &=\lim_{\Delta x\to0}\dfrac{2\left(1+x^2+\Delta x^2-2x-2\Delta x+2x\Delta x\right)-2+4x-2x^2}{\Delta x}\\[3.8pt] &=\lim_{\Delta x\to0}\dfrac{2+2x^2+2\Delta x^2-4x-4\Delta x+4x\Delta x-2+4x-2x^2}{\Delta x}\\[3.8pt] &=\lim_{\Delta x\to0}\dfrac{2\Delta x^2-4\Delta x+4x\Delta x}{\Delta x}\\[3.8pt] &=\lim_{\Delta x\to0}\left(2\Delta x-4+4x\right)\\[3.8pt] &=2(0)-4+4x\\ &=\boxed{\boxed{4x-4}} \end{aligned}$$

  5. $m=\displaystyle\lim_{\Delta x\to0}\dfrac{f(x+\Delta x)-f(x)}{\Delta x}$

    $$\begin{aligned} m&=\lim_{\Delta x\to0}\dfrac{\dfrac{2}{x+\Delta x}-\dfrac{2}{x}}{\Delta x}{\color{red}\cdot\dfrac{x(x+\Delta x)}{x(x+\Delta x)}}\\[3.8pt] &=\lim_{\Delta x\to0}\dfrac{2x-2(x+\Delta x)}{x\Delta x(x+\Delta x)}\\[3.8pt] &=\lim_{\Delta x\to0}\dfrac{2x-2x-2\Delta x}{x\Delta x(x+\Delta x)}\\[3.8pt] &=\lim_{\Delta x\to0}\dfrac{-2\Delta x}{x\Delta x(x+\Delta x)}\\[3.8pt] &=\lim_{\Delta x\to0}\dfrac{-2}{x(x+\Delta x)}\\[3.8pt] &=\dfrac{-2}{x(x+0)}\\ &=\boxed{\boxed{-\dfrac{2}{x^2}}} \end{aligned}$$
Jadi,
  1. $m = 6x - 2$
  2. $m = 3x^2 - 1$
  3. $m = 3x^2 + 3x^{-4}$
  4. $m = 4x - 4$
  5. $m=-\dfrac{2}{x^2}$

Tentukan persamaan garis singgung di titik dengan absis $x = -1$ pada kurva $f(x) = x^4$.

Buku Sekolah Digital Matematika Kelas 11 SMA K13
Pembahasan:

Misalkan $x_1 = -1$ dan $y_1 = (-1)^4 = 1$ sehingga titik singgung di $P(-1, 1)$. Jadi, gradien garis singgung adalah:

$$\begin{aligned} m_{PGS}&=\lim_{\Delta x\to0}\dfrac{f\left(x_1+\Delta x\right)-f\left(x_1\right)}{\Delta x}\\[4pt] &=\lim_{\Delta x\to0}\dfrac{f\left(-1+\Delta x\right)-f\left(-1\right)}{\Delta x}\\[4pt] &=\lim_{\Delta x\to0}\dfrac{\left(-1+\Delta x\right)^4-\left(-1\right)^4}{\Delta x} \end{aligned}$$

Ingat penjabaran $A^2 - B^2 = (A + B)(A - B)$.

$$\begin{aligned} m_{PGS}&=\lim_{\Delta x\to0}\dfrac{\left[(-1+\Delta x)^2+(-1)^2\right]\left[(-1+\Delta x)^2-(-1)^2\right]}{\Delta x}\\[4pt] &=\lim_{\Delta x\to0}\dfrac{\left[1-2\Delta x+\Delta x^2+1\right]\left[1-2\Delta x+\Delta x^2-1\right]}{\Delta x}\\[4pt] &=\lim_{\Delta x\to0}\dfrac{\left[2-2\Delta x+\Delta x^2\right]\left[-2\Delta x+\Delta x^2\right]}{\Delta x}\\[4pt] &=\lim_{\Delta x\to0}\dfrac{\left[2-2\Delta x+\Delta x^2\right]\left[-2+\Delta x\right]\Delta x}{\Delta x}\\[4pt] &=\lim_{\Delta x\to0}\left[2-2\Delta x+\Delta x^2\right]\left[-2+\Delta x\right]\\ &=\left[2-2(0)+0^2\right]\left[-2+0\right]\\ &=\left[2\right]\left[-2\right]\\ &=-4 \end{aligned}$$

Jadi, persamaan garis singgungnya adalah

$$\begin{aligned} y-y_1&=m_{PGS}\left(x-x_1\right)\\ y-1&=-4\left(x-(-1)\right)\\ y-1&=-4\left(x+1\right)\\ y-1&=-4x-4\\ y&=-4x-4+1\\ y&=\boxed{\boxed{-4x-3}} \end{aligned}$$
Jadi, persamaan garis singgung di titik dengan absis $x = -1$ pada kurva $f(x) = x^4$ adalah $y = -4x - 3$.

Tentukan $G'(x)$ jika $G(x)=\displaystyle\int_{-1}^{x^2+1}\sqrt{3+\sin(t)}\ dt$

Grup WhatsApp
Pembahasan:

Misal $u = x^2 + 1$, sehingga $u' = 2x$.

$$\begin{aligned} G'(x)&=\sqrt{3+\sin(u)}\cdot u'\\ G'(x)&=\sqrt{3+\sin\left(x^2+1\right)}\cdot (2x)\\ &=\boxed{\boxed{2x\sqrt{3+\sin\left(x^2+1\right)}}} \end{aligned}$$
Jadi, $G'(x)=2x\sqrt{3+\sin\left(x^2+1\right)}$.
