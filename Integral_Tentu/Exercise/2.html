  1. 3
  2. 4
  3. 5

  5. 7

Pembahasan:

Gunakan teknik substitusi:

Misalkan: \[u = x^2 + 1 \implies du = 2x \, dx \implies x \, dx = \dfrac{1}{2} \, du\]

Sesuaikan batas integrasi:

  • Untuk \(x = 0 \implies u = 0^2 + 1 = 1\)
  • Untuk \(x = 1 \implies u = 1^2 + 1 = 2\)

Substitusikan ke integral:

\[\begin{aligned} \int_0^1 x \cdot f(x^2 + 1) \, dx &= \int_1^2 f(u) \cdot \dfrac{1}{2} \, du \\ &= \dfrac{1}{2} \int_1^2 f(u) \, du \\ &= \dfrac{1}{2} (10) \\ &= 5 \end{aligned}\]
Jadi, nilai dari \(\displaystyle\int_0^1 x \cdot f(x^2 + 1) \, dx = 5\).

Hasil dari \(\displaystyle\int_2^4 (2x^3 + 6x^2 - 2x - 5) \, dx = \dots\)

Facebook
Pembahasan:

Tentukan antiturunan fungsi polinomial suku demi suku:

\[\begin{aligned} \int (2x^3 + 6x^2 - 2x - 5) \, dx &= \dfrac{2}{4}x^4 + \dfrac{6}{3}x^3 - \dfrac{2}{2}x^2 - 5x \\ &= \dfrac{1}{2}x^4 + 2x^3 - x^2 - 5x \end{aligned}\]

Evaluasi pada batas \(x = 2\) sampai \(x = 4\):

\[\begin{aligned} \left[\dfrac{1}{2}x^4 + 2x^3 - x^2 - 5x\right]_2^4 &= \left(\dfrac{1}{2}(4)^4 + 2(4)^3 - (4)^2 - 5(4)\right) - \left(\dfrac{1}{2}(2)^4 + 2(2)^3 - (2)^2 - 5(2)\right) \\ &= \big(128 + 128 - 16 - 20\big) - \big(8 + 16 - 4 - 10\big) \\ &= 220 - 10 \\ &= 210 \end{aligned}\]

(Koreksi perhitungan: \(4^4 = 256 \implies \frac{1}{2}(256) = 128\). Nilai evaluasi batas atas adalah \(128 + 128 - 16 - 20 = 220\), batas bawah adalah \(8 + 16 - 4 - 10 = 10\), sehingga \(220 - 10 = 210\)).

Jadi, hasil integral tersebut adalah \(210\).

Pembahasan:

Integralkan fungsi:

\[\begin{aligned} \int_1^4 (3x^{\frac{1}{2}} - 2) \, dx &= \left[3 \cdot \dfrac{2}{3}x^{\frac{3}{2}} - 2x\right]_1^4 \\ &= \left[2x\sqrt{x} - 2x\right]_1^4 \\ &= \big(2(4)\sqrt{4} - 2(4)\big) - \big(2(1)\sqrt{1} - 2(1)\big) \\ &= (16 - 8) - (2 - 2) \\ &= 8 - 0 \\ &= 8 \end{aligned}\]
Jadi, nilai integral tersebut adalah \(8\).

  2. \(-4\)
  3. \(-3\)
  4. 2

Grup Telegram
Pembahasan:

Ubah penyebut ke bentuk eksponen pecahan:

\[\dfrac{2}{x\sqrt{x}} = \dfrac{2}{x \cdot x^{\frac{1}{2}}} = \dfrac{2}{x^{\frac{3}{2}}} = 2x^{-\frac{3}{2}}\]

Integralkan:

\[\begin{aligned} \int_1^4 2x^{-\frac{3}{2}} \, dx &= \left[2 \cdot \left(\dfrac{x^{-\frac{1}{2}}}{-\frac{1}{2}}\right)\right]_1^4 \\ &= \left[-\dfrac{4}{\sqrt{x}}\right]_1^4 \\ &= \left(-\dfrac{4}{\sqrt{4}}\right) - \left(-\dfrac{4}{\sqrt{1}}\right) \\ &= -\dfrac{4}{2} - (-4) \\ &= -2 + 4 \\ &= 2 \end{aligned}\]
Jadi, hasil integral tersebut adalah \(2\).

Jadi, hasil integral tersebut adalah \(9\).

Pembahasan:

Kalikan ke dalam kurung:

\[x^2(x + 2) = x^3 + 2x^2\]

Integralkan:

\[\begin{aligned} \int_0^2 (x^3 + 2x^2) \, dx &= \left[\dfrac{1}{4}x^4 + \dfrac{2}{3}x^3\right]_0^2 \\ &= \left(\dfrac{1}{4}(2)^4 + \dfrac{2}{3}(2)^3\right) - 0 \\ &= \left(\dfrac{16}{4} + \dfrac{16}{3}\right) \\ &= 4 + 5\dfrac{1}{3} \\ &= 9\dfrac{1}{3} = \dfrac{28}{3} \end{aligned}\]
Jadi, hasil integral tersebut adalah \(9\dfrac{1}{3}\).

Pembahasan:

Gunakan substitusi aljabar:

Misalkan: \[u = 1 + x \implies x = u - 1\] \[du = dx\]

Batas integral:

  • \(x = -1 \implies u = 1 + (-1) = 0\)
  • \(x = 0 \implies u = 1 + 0 = 1\)

Substitusikan ke integral:

\[\begin{aligned} \int_{-1}^0 3x\sqrt[7]{1 + x} \, dx &= \int_0^1 3(u - 1) u^{\frac{1}{7}} \, du \\ &= \int_0^1 \left(3u^{\frac{8}{7}} - 3u^{\frac{1}{7}}\right) du \\ &= \left[3 \cdot \dfrac{7}{15}u^{\frac{15}{7}} - 3 \cdot \dfrac{7}{8}u^{\frac{8}{7}}\right]_0^1 \\ &= \left[\dfrac{7}{5}u^{\frac{15}{7}} - \dfrac{21}{8}u^{\frac{8}{7}}\right]_0^1 \\ &= \left(\dfrac{7}{5}(1) - \dfrac{21}{8}(1)\right) - 0 \\ &= \dfrac{56 - 105}{40} \\ &= -\dfrac{49}{40} = -\dfrac{147}{120} \end{aligned}\]
Jadi, hasil integral tersebut adalah \(-\dfrac{147}{120}\).

Pembahasan:

Gunakan substitusi:

Misalkan: \[u = 9x^2 + 6x + 1 \implies du = (18x + 6) \, dx\]

Batas integral:

  • \(x = 0 \implies u = 9(0)^2 + 6(0) + 1 = 1\)
  • \(x = 1 \implies u = 9(1)^2 + 6(1) + 1 = 16\)

Substitusikan ke integral:

\[\begin{aligned} \int_0^1 \dfrac{10(18x + 6)}{\sqrt{9x^2 + 6x + 1}} \, dx &= \int_1^{16} \dfrac{10}{\sqrt{u}} \, du \\ &= 10 \int_1^{16} u^{-\frac{1}{2}} \, du \\ &= \left[10 \cdot 2\sqrt{u}\right]_1^{16} \\ &= \left[20\sqrt{u}\right]_1^{16} \\ &= 20\sqrt{16} - 20\sqrt{1} \\ &= 20(4) - 20(1) \\ &= 80 - 20 \\ &= 60 \end{aligned}\]
Jadi, hasil integral tersebut adalah \(60\).

Pembahasan:

Gunakan teknik substitusi aljabar:

Misalkan: \[u = 1 + x \implies x = u - 1\] \[du = dx\]

Batas integrasi:

  • \(x = 0 \implies u = 1 + 0 = 1\)
  • \(x = 3 \implies u = 1 + 3 = 4\)

Substitusikan ke integral:

\[\begin{aligned} \int_0^3 x\sqrt{1 + x} \, dx &= \int_1^4 (u - 1)\sqrt{u} \, du \\ &= \int_1^4 \left(u^{\frac{3}{2}} - u^{\frac{1}{2}}\right) du \\ &= \left[\dfrac{2}{5}u^{\frac{5}{2}} - \dfrac{2}{3}u^{\frac{3}{2}}\right]_1^4 \\ &= \left(\dfrac{2}{5}(4)^{\frac{5}{2}} - \dfrac{2}{3}(4)^{\frac{3}{2}}\right) - \left(\dfrac{2}{5}(1)^{\frac{5}{2}} - \dfrac{2}{3}(1)^{\frac{3}{2}}\right) \\ &= \left(\dfrac{2}{5}(32) - \dfrac{2}{3}(8)\right) - \left(\dfrac{2}{5} - \dfrac{2}{3}\right) \\ &= \left(\dfrac{64}{5} - \dfrac{16}{3}\right) - \left(-\dfrac{4}{15}\right) \\ &= \left(\dfrac{192 - 80}{15}\right) + \dfrac{4}{15} \\ &= \dfrac{112}{15} + \dfrac{4}{15} \\ &= \dfrac{116}{15} \end{aligned}\]
Jadi, hasil dari \(\displaystyle\int_0^3 x\sqrt{1+x} \, dx = \dfrac{116}{15}\).
