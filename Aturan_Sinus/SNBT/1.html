Berikut adalah soal tingkat ... materi Aturan Sinus

Diketahui segitiga \(ABC\) memiliki panjang sisi \(BC = 8\text{ cm}\), besar sudut \(\angle BAC = 45^\circ\), dan \(\angle ABC = 105^\circ\). Panjang sisi \(AC\) adalah ....
  1. \(4(\sqrt{3} + 1)\text{ cm}\)
  2. \(4(\sqrt{6} + 1)\text{ cm}\)
  3. \(8(\sqrt{3} - 1)\text{ cm}\)
  4. \(2(\sqrt{6} + \sqrt{2})\text{ cm}\)
  5. \(8(\sqrt{2} + 1)\text{ cm}\)
SNBT / UTBK
Pembahasan:
Pada segitiga \(ABC\), jumlah ketiga sudutnya adalah \(180^\circ\): \[\begin{aligned} \angle ACB &= 180^\circ - (\angle BAC + \angle ABC) \\ &= 180^\circ - (45^\circ + 105^\circ) \\ &= 180^\circ - 150^\circ = 30^\circ \end{aligned}\] Langkah 1: Menentukan nilai \(\sin 105^\circ\)

Gunakan rumus sinus jumlah dua sudut: \[\begin{aligned} \sin 105^\circ &= \sin (60^\circ + 45^\circ) \\ &= \sin 60^\circ \cos 45^\circ + \cos 60^\circ \sin 45^\circ \\ &= \left(\dfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{3}\right)\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{2}\right) + \left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{2}\right) \\ &= \dfrac{\sqrt{6} + \sqrt{2}}{4} \end{aligned}\]

Langkah 2: Menerapkan aturan sinus

Pada \(\triangle ABC\), berlaku aturan sinus: \[\dfrac{AC}{\sin \angle ABC} = \dfrac{BC}{\sin \angle BAC}\] Substitusikan nilai-nilai yang diketahui: \[\begin{aligned} \dfrac{AC}{\sin 105^\circ} &= \dfrac{8}{\sin 45^\circ} \\[6pt] AC &= \dfrac{8 \cdot \sin 105^\circ}{\sin 45^\circ} \\[6pt] AC &= \dfrac{8 \cdot \left(\dfrac{\sqrt{6} + \sqrt{2}}{4}\right)}{\dfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{2}} \\[6pt] AC &= \dfrac{2(\sqrt{6} + \sqrt{2})}{\dfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{2}} \\[6pt] AC &= \dfrac{4(\sqrt{6} + \sqrt{2})}{\sqrt{2}} \\[6pt] AC &= 4\left(\dfrac{\sqrt{6}}{\sqrt{2}} + \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{\sqrt{2}}\right) \\[6pt] AC &= 4(\sqrt{3} + 1)\text{ cm} \end{aligned}\]

Jadi, panjang sisi \(AC\) adalah \(4(\sqrt{3} + 1)\text{ cm}\).
