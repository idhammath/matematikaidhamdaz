ALTERNATIF PENYELESAIAN
\begin{aligned} \left(g\circ f\right)(x) &= x + \dfrac6a\\ g\left(f(x)\right)&=\dfrac{ax}a+ \dfrac6a\\ g\left(ax + 6\right)&=\dfrac{ax+6}a\\ g(x)&=\dfrac{x}a\\ 6(6a)&=\dfrac{6a}a\\ &=\boxed{\boxed{6}} \end{aligned}
Jadi, g(6a) = 6.
