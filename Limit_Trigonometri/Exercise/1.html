\[\lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{\cos 3x-\cos x}{\tan^2 2x}=\dots\]

  1. \(1\)
  2. \(-\dfrac{1}{2}\)
  3. \(-1\)
  4. \(\dfrac{1}{2}\)
  5. \(3\)
Pembahasan:

Gunakan rumus selisih kosinus \(\cos A - \cos B = -2\sin\left(\dfrac{A+B}{2}\right)\sin\left(\dfrac{A-B}{2}\right)\):

\[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{\cos 3x-\cos x}{\tan^2 2x} &= \lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{-2\sin\left(\dfrac{3x+x}{2}\right)\sin\left(\dfrac{3x-x}{2}\right)}{\tan^2 2x}\\[3.7pt] &= \lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{-2\sin 2x\sin x}{\tan^2 2x}\\[3.7pt] &= -2\cdot\lim_{x\to0}\left(\dfrac{\sin 2x}{\tan 2x}\cdot\dfrac{\sin x}{\tan 2x}\right)\\[3.7pt] &= -2\cdot\left(\dfrac{2}{2}\right)\cdot\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right) = -1 \end{aligned} \]
Jadi, \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{\cos 3x-\cos x}{\tan^2 2x}=-1\).

\[\lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{1-\cos x}{\sin x}=\dots\]

  1. \(0\)
  2. \(\dfrac{1}{4}\)
  3. \(\dfrac{1}{2}\)
  4. \(1\)
  5. \(2\)
Pembahasan:

Cara 1: Mengalikan dengan Sekawan

\[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{1-\cos x}{\sin x} &= \lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{1-\cos x}{\sin x}\cdot\dfrac{1+\cos x}{1+\cos x}\\[3.7pt] &= \lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{1-\cos^2 x}{\sin x(1+\cos x)}\\[3.7pt] &= \lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{\sin^2 x}{\sin x(1+\cos x)}\\[3.7pt] &= \lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{\sin x}{1+\cos x}\\[3.7pt] &= \dfrac{\sin 0}{1+\cos 0} = \dfrac{0}{1+1} = 0 \end{aligned} \]

Cara 2: Sudut Paruh

\[ \lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{2\sin^2\left(\frac{1}{2}x\right)}{2\sin\left(\frac{1}{2}x\right)\cos\left(\frac{1}{2}x\right)} = \lim_{x\to0}\tan\left(\frac{1}{2}x\right) = 0 \]
Jadi, \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{1-\cos x}{\sin x}=0\).

Nilai dari: \[\lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{2\sin^2\left(\frac{1}{2}x\right)}{x\tan x}=\dots\]

  1. \(-2\)
  2. \(-1\)
  3. \(-\dfrac{1}{2}\)
  4. \(\dfrac{1}{2}\)
  5. \(1\)
Pembahasan:

Gunakan sifat dasar limit trigonometri \(\lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{\sin ax}{x} = a\) dan \(\lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{\sin ax}{\tan bx} = \dfrac{a}{b}\):

\[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{2\sin^2\left(\frac{1}{2}x\right)}{x\tan x} &= 2\cdot\lim_{x\to0}\left(\dfrac{\sin\left(\frac{1}{2}x\right)}{x}\cdot\dfrac{\sin\left(\frac{1}{2}x\right)}{\tan x}\right)\\[3.7pt] &= 2\cdot\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)\cdot\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)\\[3.7pt] &= \dfrac{1}{2} \end{aligned} \]
Jadi, hasilnya adalah \(\dfrac{1}{2}\).
Jawaban: D

Nilai dari: \[\lim_{x\to-2}\dfrac{\left(x^2-4\right)\tan(x+2)}{\sin^2(x+2)}=\dots\]

  1. \(-4\)
  2. \(-3\)
  3. \(0\)
  4. \(4\)
  5. \(\infty\)
Pembahasan:

Faktorkan \(x^2 - 4 = (x - 2)(x + 2)\):

\[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{x\to-2}\dfrac{\left(x^2-4\right)\tan(x+2)}{\sin^2(x+2)} &= \lim_{x\to-2}\left[(x-2)\cdot\dfrac{x+2}{\sin(x+2)}\cdot\dfrac{\tan(x+2)}{\sin(x+2)}\right] \end{aligned} \] Misalkan \(u = x + 2\), saat \(x \to -2\) maka \(u \to 0\): \[ \begin{aligned} &= (-2 - 2)\cdot\left(\lim_{u\to0}\dfrac{u}{\sin u}\right)\cdot\left(\lim_{u\to0}\dfrac{\tan u}{\sin u}\right)\\[3.7pt] &= (-4)\cdot 1 \cdot 1 = -4 \end{aligned} \]
Jadi, hasilnya adalah \(-4\).
Jawaban: A

\[\lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{\sqrt{1+\tan x}-\sqrt{1+\sin x}}{x^3}=\dots\]

Pembahasan:

Kalikan pembilang dan penyebut dengan bentuk sekawan dari pembilang:

\[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{\sqrt{1+\tan x}-\sqrt{1+\sin x}}{x^3} &= \lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{(1+\tan x)-(1+\sin x)}{x^3\left(\sqrt{1+\tan x}+\sqrt{1+\sin x}\right)}\\[3.7pt] &= \lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{\tan x - \sin x}{x^3\left(\sqrt{1+\tan x}+\sqrt{1+\sin x}\right)} \end{aligned} \] Ubah \(\tan x - \sin x = \tan x(1 - \cos x) = \tan x \cdot 2\sin^2\left(\frac{1}{2}x\right)\): \[ \begin{aligned} &= \lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{\tan x\cdot 2\sin^2\left(\frac{1}{2}x\right)}{x^3}\cdot\lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{1+\tan x}+\sqrt{1+\sin x}}\\[3.7pt] &= \left(1 \cdot 2\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^2\right)\cdot\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{1+0}+\sqrt{1+0}}\\[3.7pt] &= \left(2\cdot\dfrac{1}{4}\right)\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}\\[3.7pt] &= \dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{1}{2} = \dfrac{1}{4} \end{aligned} \]
Jadi, \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{\sqrt{1+\tan x}-\sqrt{1+\sin x}}{x^3}=\dfrac{1}{4}\).

\[\lim_{x\to2}\dfrac{\sin(2x-4)}{2-\sqrt{6-x}}=\dots\]

  1. \(-8\)
  2. \(-2\)
  3. \(0\)
  4. \(2\)
  5. \(8\)
Pembahasan:

Cara 1: Mengalikan dengan Sekawan

\[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{x\to2}\dfrac{\sin(2x-4)}{2-\sqrt{6-x}} &= \lim_{x\to2}\dfrac{\sin 2(x-2)}{2-\sqrt{6-x}}\cdot\dfrac{2+\sqrt{6-x}}{2+\sqrt{6-x}}\\[3.7pt] &= \lim_{x\to2}\dfrac{\sin 2(x-2)\left(2+\sqrt{6-x}\right)}{4-(6-x)}\\[3.7pt] &= \lim_{x\to2}\dfrac{\sin 2(x-2)\left(2+\sqrt{6-x}\right)}{x-2}\\[3.7pt] &= \lim_{x\to2}\dfrac{\sin 2(x-2)}{x-2}\cdot\left(2+\sqrt{6-x}\right)\\[3.7pt] &= 2\cdot\left(2+\sqrt{6-2}\right) = 2(2+2) = 8 \end{aligned} \]

Cara 2: Aturan L'Hôpital

\[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{x\to2}\dfrac{2\cos(2x-4)}{-\dfrac{-1}{2\sqrt{6-x}}} &= \lim_{x\to2}4\cos(2x-4)\sqrt{6-x}\\[3.7pt] &= 4\cos(0)\sqrt{4} = 4(1)(2) = 8 \end{aligned} \]
Jadi, hasilnya adalah \(8\).

\[\lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{\tan 5x-\tan 3x-\tan 2x}{x^3}=\dots\]

Grup WhatsApp
Pembahasan:

Gunakan rumus pengurangan tangen \(\tan(A - B) = \dfrac{\tan A - \tan B}{1 + \tan A\tan B}\) dengan \(A = 5x\) dan \(B = 3x\):

\[ \begin{aligned} \tan(5x - 3x) &= \dfrac{\tan 5x - \tan 3x}{1 + \tan 5x\tan 3x}\\[3.7pt] \tan 2x(1 + \tan 5x\tan 3x) &= \tan 5x - \tan 3x\\[3.7pt] \tan 5x - \tan 3x - \tan 2x &= \tan 2x\tan 3x\tan 5x \end{aligned} \] Substitusikan kembali ke bentuk limit: \[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{\tan 5x-\tan 3x-\tan 2x}{x^3} &= \lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{\tan 2x\tan 3x\tan 5x}{x^3}\\[3.7pt] &= \left(\lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{\tan 2x}{x}\right)\cdot\left(\lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{\tan 3x}{x}\right)\cdot\left(\lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{\tan 5x}{x}\right)\\[3.7pt] &= 2 \cdot 3 \cdot 5 = 30 \end{aligned} \]
Jadi, \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{\tan 5x-\tan 3x-\tan 2x}{x^3}=30\).

\[\lim_{x\to-1}\dfrac{\sin(2x+2)}{\sqrt{3x+4}-\sqrt{x+2}}=\dots\]

  1. \(\dfrac{1}{2}\)
  2. \(1\)
  3. \(2\)
  4. \(4\)
  5. \(6\)
Pembahasan:

Kalikan penyebut dengan bentuk sekawannya:

\[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{x\to-1}\dfrac{\sin 2(x+1)}{\sqrt{3x+4}-\sqrt{x+2}} &= \lim_{x\to-1}\dfrac{\sin 2(x+1)}{\sqrt{3x+4}-\sqrt{x+2}}\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt{3x+4}+\sqrt{x+2}}{\sqrt{3x+4}+\sqrt{x+2}}\\[3.7pt] &= \lim_{x\to-1}\dfrac{\sin 2(x+1)\left(\sqrt{3x+4}+\sqrt{x+2}\right)}{(3x+4)-(x+2)}\\[3.7pt] &= \lim_{x\to-1}\dfrac{\sin 2(x+1)\left(\sqrt{3x+4}+\sqrt{x+2}\right)}{2x+2}\\[3.7pt] &= \lim_{x\to-1}\dfrac{\sin 2(x+1)}{2(x+1)}\cdot\left(\sqrt{3x+4}+\sqrt{x+2}\right)\\[3.7pt] &= 1\cdot\left(\sqrt{3(-1)+4}+\sqrt{-1+2}\right)\\[3.7pt] &= \sqrt{1}+\sqrt{1} = 2 \end{aligned} \]
Jadi, hasilnya adalah \(2\).
Jawaban: C

\[\lim_{x\to3}\dfrac{\sin\left(\sqrt{x+1}-2\right)}{x-3}=\dots\]

Grup Telegram
Pembahasan:

Kalikan pembilang dan penyebut dengan \(\dfrac{\sqrt{x+1}-2}{\sqrt{x+1}-2}\):

\[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{x\to3}\dfrac{\sin\left(\sqrt{x+1}-2\right)}{x-3} &= \lim_{x\to3}\left[\dfrac{\sin\left(\sqrt{x+1}-2\right)}{\sqrt{x+1}-2}\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt{x+1}-2}{x-3}\right]\\[3.7pt] &= 1\cdot\lim_{x\to3}\left[\dfrac{\sqrt{x+1}-2}{x-3}\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt{x+1}+2}{\sqrt{x+1}+2}\right]\\[3.7pt] &= \lim_{x\to3}\dfrac{(x+1)-4}{(x-3)\left(\sqrt{x+1}+2\right)}\\[3.7pt] &= \lim_{x\to3}\dfrac{x-3}{(x-3)\left(\sqrt{x+1}+2\right)}\\[3.7pt] &= \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3+1}+2} = \dfrac{1}{2+2} = \dfrac{1}{4} \end{aligned} \]
Jadi, hasilnya adalah \(\dfrac{1}{4}\).

Nilai dari: \[\lim_{x\to\pi}\left(\sin x+\cos x\right)=\dots\]

  1. \(-1\)
  2. \(1\)
  3. \(-2\)
  4. \(0\)
  5. \(2\)
Pembahasan:

Gunakan teknik substitusi langsung karena fungsi trigonometri kontinu pada titik \(x = \pi\):

\[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{x\to\pi}(\sin x + \cos x) &= \sin\pi + \cos\pi\\[3.7pt] &= 0 + (-1) = -1 \end{aligned} \]
Jadi, hasilnya adalah \(-1\).
Jawaban: A
