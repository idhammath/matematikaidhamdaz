Hasil dari \(\displaystyle\int\sin^3x \, dx\) adalah ....

  1. \(-\dfrac{1}{3}\cos^3x + C\)
  2. \(-\cos x - \dfrac{1}{3}\sin^3x + C\)
  3. \(\cos x - \dfrac{1}{3}\sin^3x + C\)
  4. \(-\cos x + \dfrac{1}{3}\cos^3x + C\)
  5. \(-\dfrac{1}{3}\sin^3x + C\)
Pembahasan:

Uraikan \(\sin^3x\) menggunakan identitas trigonometri \(\sin^2x = 1 - \cos^2x\):

\[\begin{aligned} \int\sin^3x \, dx &= \int\sin x\sin^2x \, dx \\ &= \int\sin x\left(1-\cos^2x\right) \, dx \\ &= \int\left(\sin x-\sin x\cos^2x\right) \, dx \\ &= \int \sin x \, dx - \int\sin x\cos^2x \, dx \end{aligned}\]

Gunakan teknik substitusi untuk bagian \(\displaystyle\int\sin x\cos^2x \, dx\):

Misalkan: \[u = \cos x \implies du = -\sin x \, dx \implies dx = \dfrac{du}{-\sin x}\]

Sehingga diperoleh:

\[\begin{aligned} \int \sin x \, dx - \int\sin x\cos^2x \, dx &= -\cos x - \int\sin x \cdot u^2 \cdot \dfrac{du}{-\sin x} \\[8pt] &= -\cos x + \int u^2 \, du \\ &= -\cos x + \dfrac{1}{3}u^3 + C \\ &= -\cos x + \dfrac{1}{3}\cos^3x + C \end{aligned}\]
Jadi, \(\displaystyle\int\sin^3x \, dx = -\cos x + \dfrac{1}{3}\cos^3x + C\).
JAWABAN: D

Hasil dari \(\displaystyle\int\sin x\left(1-\sin^2x\right) \, dx = \dots\)

  1. \(\cos x - \dfrac{1}{3}\cos^3x + C\)
  2. \(\cos x - \dfrac{1}{3}\sin^3x + C\)
  3. \(-\dfrac{1}{3}\cos^3x + C\)
  4. \(-\dfrac{1}{3}\sin^3x + C\)
  5. \(-\cos x - \dfrac{1}{3}\sin^3x + C\)
Pembahasan:

Gunakan identitas trigonometri \(1 - \sin^2x = \cos^2x\):

\[\int\sin x\left(1-\sin^2x\right) \, dx = \int\sin x\cos^2x \, dx\]

Gunakan teknik substitusi:

Misalkan: \[u = \cos x \implies du = -\sin x \, dx \implies dx = \dfrac{du}{-\sin x}\]

Substitusikan ke dalam integral:

\[\begin{aligned} \int\sin x\cos^2x \, dx &= \int\sin x \cdot u^2 \cdot \dfrac{du}{-\sin x} \\[8pt] &= -\int u^2 \, du \\ &= -\dfrac{1}{3}u^3 + C \\ &= -\dfrac{1}{3}\cos^3x + C \end{aligned}\]
Jadi, \(\displaystyle\int\sin x\left(1-\sin^2x\right) \, dx = -\dfrac{1}{3}\cos^3x + C\).
JAWABAN: C

Hasil dari \(\displaystyle\int 6\sec^2(2\pi - 4x) \, dx = \dots\)

SKB CPNS MATEMATIKA 2020
Pembahasan:

Gunakan rumus dasar integral trigonometri:

\[\int \sec^2(ax + b) \, dx = \dfrac{1}{a}\tan(ax + b) + C\]

Dengan \(a = -4\) dan \(b = 2\pi\), diperoleh:

\[\begin{aligned} \int 6\sec^2(2\pi - 4x) \, dx &= 6 \cdot \left(\dfrac{1}{-4}\right)\tan(2\pi - 4x) + C \\ &= -\dfrac{6}{4}\tan(2\pi - 4x) + C \\ &= -\dfrac{3}{2}\tan(2\pi - 4x) + C \end{aligned}\]
Jadi, \(\displaystyle\int 6\sec^2(2\pi - 4x) \, dx = -\dfrac{3}{2}\tan(2\pi - 4x) + C\).
