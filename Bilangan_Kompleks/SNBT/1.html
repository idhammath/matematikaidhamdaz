  1. \(-16\)

  4. \(16i\)

Pembahasan:
Soal ini dapat diselesaikan dengan dua pendekatan: Metode 1: Aljabar Perpangkatan Bertahap

1. Menghitung pembilang \((1 + i\sqrt{3})^6\): \[\begin{aligned} (1 + i\sqrt{3})^2 &= 1^2 + 2(1)(i\sqrt{3}) + (i\sqrt{3})^2 \\ &= 1 + 2i\sqrt{3} + 3i^2 \\ &= 1 + 2i\sqrt{3} - 3 \\ &= -2 + 2i\sqrt{3} = 2(-1 + i\sqrt{3}) \end{aligned}\] Hitung pangkat tiga: \[\begin{aligned} (1 + i\sqrt{3})^3 &= (1 + i\sqrt{3})(1 + i\sqrt{3})^2 \\ &= (1 + i\sqrt{3})(-2 + 2i\sqrt{3}) \\ &= -2 + 2i\sqrt{3} - 2i\sqrt{3} + 2i^2(3) \\ &= -2 + 6(-1) = -8 \end{aligned}\] Maka: \[(1 + i\sqrt{3})^6 = \left[(1 + i\sqrt{3})^3\right]^2 = (-8)^2 = 64\]

2. Menghitung penyebut \((1 - i)^4\): \[\begin{aligned} (1 - i)^2 &= 1^2 - 2i + i^2 \\ &= 1 - 2i - 1 = -2i \end{aligned}\] Maka: \[(1 - i)^4 = \left[(1 - i)^2\right]^2 = (-2i)^2 = 4i^2 = 4(-1) = -4\]

3. Menghitung nilai \(z\): \[z = \dfrac{64}{-4} = -16\]

Metode 2: Bentuk Polar dan Teorema De Moivre

Untuk \(1 + i\sqrt{3}\): Modulus \(r_1 = \sqrt{1^2 + (\sqrt{3})^2} = 2\), argumen \(\theta_1 = 60^\circ = \dfrac{\pi}{3}\). \[1 + i\sqrt{3} = 2\left(\cos \dfrac{\pi}{3} + i\sin \dfrac{\pi}{3}\right)\] Berdasarkan Teorema De Moivre: \[\begin{aligned} (1 + i\sqrt{3})^6 &= 2^6 \left(\cos \left(6 \cdot \dfrac{\pi}{3}\right) + i\sin \left(6 \cdot \dfrac{\pi}{3}\right)\right) \\ &= 64 (\cos 2\pi + i\sin 2\pi) = 64(1 + 0) = 64 \end{aligned}\] Untuk \(1 - i\): Modulus \(r_2 = \sqrt{1^2 + (-1)^2} = \sqrt{2}\), argumen \(\theta_2 = -\dfrac{\pi}{4}\). \[\begin{aligned} (1 - i)^4 &= (\sqrt{2})^4 \left(\cos \left(4 \cdot \left(-\dfrac{\pi}{4}\right)\right) + i\sin \left(4 \cdot \left(-\dfrac{\pi}{4}\right)\right)\right) \\ &= 4 (\cos (-\pi) + i\sin (-\pi)) = 4(-1 + 0) = -4 \end{aligned}\] Sehingga: \[z = \dfrac{64}{-4} = -16\]

Jadi, nilai dari bilangan kompleks \(z\) adalah \(-16\).
