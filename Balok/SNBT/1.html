Balok ABCD.EFGH alasnya persegi dengan AB = 4 cm, AE = 8 cm. Jika θ adalah sudut antara bidang ACH dan bidang ABCD, maka sin θ = ....
  1. \dfrac45\sqrt2
  2. \dfrac23\sqrt2
  3. \dfrac12\sqrt2
  1. \dfrac47\sqrt2
  2. \dfrac13
SUMBER
ALTERNATIF PENYELESAIAN
θ = ∠ DOH

BD=4\sqrt2 \begin{aligned} DO&=\dfrac12BD\\[3.5pt] &=\dfrac12\left(4\sqrt2\right)\\[3.5pt] &=2\sqrt2 \end{aligned} \begin{aligned} HO&=\sqrt{HD^2+DO^2}\\ &=\sqrt{8^2+\left(2\sqrt2\right)^2}\\ &=\sqrt{64+8}\\ &=\sqrt{72}\\ &=6\sqrt2 \end{aligned}
\begin{aligned} \sin\theta&=\dfrac{HD}{HO}\\[3.5pt] &=\dfrac8{6\sqrt2}\\[3.5pt] &=\dfrac4{3\sqrt2}&\times\dfrac{\sqrt2}{\sqrt2}\\[3.5pt] &=\dfrac46\sqrt2\\ &=\boxed{\boxed{\dfrac23\sqrt2}} \end{aligned}
Jadi, \sin\theta =\dfrac23\sqrt2.
