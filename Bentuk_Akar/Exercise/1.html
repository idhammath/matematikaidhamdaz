Pecahan \(\dfrac{\sqrt5+\sqrt3-\sqrt2}{\sqrt2+\sqrt3-\sqrt5}\) setara dengan ...
  1. \(\dfrac{\sqrt{60}+\sqrt6}2\)
  2. \(\dfrac{\sqrt{10}+\sqrt6}2\)
  3. \(\dfrac{\sqrt{15}+\sqrt3}2\)
  4. \(\dfrac{\sqrt{15}-\sqrt3}2\)
  5. \(\dfrac{\sqrt{15}+\sqrt6}2\)
Grup Matematika Zone Indonesia
Pembahasan:

Kalikan pembilang dan penyebut dengan bentuk sekawan penyebutnya, yaitu \((\sqrt2+\sqrt3)+\sqrt5\):

\[ \begin{aligned} \dfrac{\sqrt5+\sqrt3-\sqrt2}{\sqrt2+\sqrt3-\sqrt5} &= \dfrac{\sqrt5+(\sqrt3-\sqrt2)}{(\sqrt2+\sqrt3)-\sqrt5} \cdot \dfrac{(\sqrt2+\sqrt3)+\sqrt5}{(\sqrt2+\sqrt3)+\sqrt5} \\[6pt] &= \dfrac{\sqrt{10}+\sqrt{15}+5+\sqrt6+3+\sqrt{15}-2-\sqrt6-\sqrt{10}}{(\sqrt2+\sqrt3)^2 - (\sqrt5)^2} \\[6pt] &= \dfrac{2\sqrt{15}+6}{(2+2\sqrt6+3)-5} \\[6pt] &= \dfrac{2\sqrt{15}+6}{2\sqrt6} = \dfrac{\sqrt{15}+3}{\sqrt6} \\[6pt] &= \dfrac{\sqrt{15}+3}{\sqrt6} \cdot \dfrac{\sqrt6}{\sqrt6} = \dfrac{\sqrt{90}+3\sqrt6}{6} \\[6pt] &= \dfrac{3\sqrt{10}+3\sqrt6}{6} = \boxed{\boxed{\dfrac{\sqrt{10}+\sqrt6}{2}}} \end{aligned} \]
Jadi, \(\dfrac{\sqrt5+\sqrt3-\sqrt2}{\sqrt2+\sqrt3-\sqrt5}\) setara dengan \(\dfrac{\sqrt{10}+\sqrt6}2\).

Sederhanakan terlebih dahulu nilai \(r\):

\[ \begin{aligned} r &= \dfrac{5(4\sqrt2-5)}{10(1+2\sqrt2)(2-\sqrt2)} \\[6pt] &= \dfrac{4\sqrt2-5}{2(2-\sqrt2+4\sqrt2-4)} \\[6pt] &= \dfrac{4\sqrt2-5}{2(3\sqrt2-2)} \cdot \dfrac{3\sqrt2+2}{3\sqrt2+2} \\[6pt] &= \dfrac{(4\sqrt2-5)(3\sqrt2+2)}{2((3\sqrt2)^2-2^2)} \\[6pt] &= \dfrac{24+8\sqrt2-15\sqrt2-10}{2(18-4)} \\[6pt] &= \dfrac{14-7\sqrt2}{2(14)} = \dfrac{7(2-\sqrt2)}{28} = \dfrac{2-\sqrt2}{4} \end{aligned} \]

Substitusikan nilai \(r\) ke dalam ekspresi yang ditanyakan:

\[ \begin{aligned} (4r-2)^2 &= \left(4 \cdot \dfrac{2-\sqrt2}{4} - 2\right)^2 \\ &= (2-\sqrt2-2)^2 \\ &= (-\sqrt2)^2 = \boxed{\boxed{2}} \end{aligned} \]
Jadi, \((4r-2)^2 = 2\).
