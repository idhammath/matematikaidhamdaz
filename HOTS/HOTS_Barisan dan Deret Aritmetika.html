ALTERNATIF PENYELESAIAN
\begin{aligned} a_{10}&=182\\ a+9b&=182 \end{aligned} \begin{aligned} a_2+a_4+a_6+\cdots+a_{20}&=2000\\ a+b+a+3b+a+5b+\cdots+a+19b&=2000\\ 10a+(1+3+5+\cdots+19)b&=2000\\ 10a+100b&=2000\\ a+10b&=200 \end{aligned} \begin{aligned} a+10b&=200\\ a+9b&=182&\ -\\\hline b&=18 \end{aligned}
\begin{aligned} a+9b&=182\\ a+9(18)&=182\\ a+162&=182\\ a&=20 \end{aligned} \begin{aligned} a_6&=a+5b\\ &=20+5(18)\\ &=110 \end{aligned}
Jadi, a6 = 110.

OSK 2019
ALTERNATIF PENYELESAIAN

\begin{aligned} x_1 + x_2 +\cdots+ x_{400}&=S_{400}\\ &=\dfrac{n}2\left(2a+(n-1)b\right)\\[4pt] &=\dfrac{400}2\left(2(1)+(400-1)\dfrac12\right)\\[4pt] &=200\left(2+\dfrac{399}2\right)\\[4pt] &=400+39900\\ &=\boxed{\boxed{40300}} \end{aligned}
Jadi, x1 + x2 + ⋯ + x400 = 40300.

OSK SMA 2024

\(\begin{aligned} \dfrac{1+2+3+\cdots+104}{3+4+5+\cdots+106}&=\dfrac{\dfrac12(104)(1+104)}{\dfrac12(104)(3+106)}\\[4pt] &=\dfrac{105}{109} \end{aligned}\)

105 + 109 = 214
Jadi, a + b = 214.
