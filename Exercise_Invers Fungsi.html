Tentukan f−1(x) dari \({f(x)=\dfrac{3x+5}{2x-3}}\)
Grup Matematika Idhamdaz
ALTERNATIF PENYELESAIAN
\begin{aligned} f(x)&=\dfrac{3x+5}{2x-3}\\[3.5pt] y&=\dfrac{3x+5}{2x-3}\\[3.5pt] (2x-3)y&=3x+5\\ 2xy-3y&=3x+5\\ 2xy-3x&=3y+5\\ x(2y-3)&=3y+5\\ x&=\dfrac{3y+5}{2y-3}\\ f^{-1}(x)&=\boxed{\boxed{\dfrac{3x+5}{2x-3}}} \end{aligned}
Jadi, \(f^{-1}(x)=\dfrac{3x+5}{2x-3}\).
