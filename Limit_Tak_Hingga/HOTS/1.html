  5. \(3\)

Pembahasan:

Ubah basis pecahan menjadi eksponen negatif berbasis 2:

\[ \left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{3x} + \left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^x = 2^{-3x} + 2^{-x} = 2^{-x}\left(2^{-2x} + 1\right) \] Substitusikan ke dalam bentuk limit: \[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{x\to\infty}\left(\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{3x}+\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^x\right)^{\frac{1}{x^2}} &= \lim_{x\to\infty}\left[2^{-x}\left(1 + 2^{-2x}\right)\right]^{\frac{1}{x^2}}\\[3.7pt] &= \lim_{x\to\infty}\left[\left(2^{-x}\right)^{\frac{1}{x^2}} \cdot \left(1 + 2^{-2x}\right)^{\frac{1}{x^2}}\right]\\[3.7pt] &= \lim_{x\to\infty}\left[2^{-\frac{1}{x}} \cdot \left(1 + 2^{-2x}\right)^{\frac{1}{x^2}}\right] \end{aligned} \] Saat \(x \to \infty\), maka \(-\frac{1}{x} \to 0\) dan \(2^{-2x} \to 0\): \[ 2^{-0} \cdot (1 + 0)^0 = 1 \cdot 1 = 1 \]
Jadi, \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to\infty}\left(\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{3x}+\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^x\right)^{\frac{1}{x^2}}=1\).

\[\lim_{n\to\infty}\dfrac{\sqrt[n]{8}-1}{\sqrt[n]{16}-1}=\dots\]

Mathisstillfun
Pembahasan:

Nyatakan pembilang dan penyebut dalam bentuk pangkat dari \(\sqrt[n]{2}\):

\[ \sqrt[n]{8} = \left(\sqrt[n]{2}\right)^3 \quad \text{dan} \quad \sqrt[n]{16} = \left(\sqrt[n]{2}\right)^4 \] Misalkan \(p = \sqrt[n]{2} = 2^{\frac{1}{n}}\). Saat \(n \to \infty\), maka \(\frac{1}{n} \to 0\), sehingga \(p \to 2^0 = 1\). Maka bentuk limit bertransformasi menjadi: \[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{p\to1}\dfrac{p^3-1}{p^4-1} &= \lim_{p\to1}\dfrac{(p-1)\left(p^2+p+1\right)}{(p-1)(p+1)\left(p^2+1\right)}\\[3.7pt] &= \lim_{p\to1}\dfrac{p^2+p+1}{(p+1)\left(p^2+1\right)}\\[3.7pt] &= \dfrac{1^2+1+1}{(1+1)(1^2+1)}\\[3.7pt] &= \dfrac{3}{2 \cdot 2} = \dfrac{3}{4} \end{aligned} \]
Jadi, \(\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty}\dfrac{\sqrt[n]{8}-1}{\sqrt[n]{16}-1}=\dfrac{3}{4}\).

Pembahasan:

Uraikan \(5^{x-1} = \dfrac{5^x}{5}\), lalu lakukan substitusi variabel:

Misalkan \(y = 5^{-x} = \dfrac{1}{5^x}\). Saat \(x \to +\infty\), maka \(y \to 0^+\).

\[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{x\to+\infty}\left(5^{x-1}\tan\left(\dfrac{3}{5^x}\right)\right) &= \lim_{x\to+\infty}\dfrac{\tan\left(3\cdot 5^{-x}\right)}{5\cdot 5^{-x}}\\[3.7pt] &= \lim_{y\to0}\dfrac{\tan(3y)}{5y}\\[3.7pt] &= \dfrac{3}{5} \end{aligned} \]
Jadi, hasilnya adalah \(\dfrac{3}{5}\).
