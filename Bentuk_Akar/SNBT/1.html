Jika a dan b adalah bilangan real positif, maka \dfrac{\left(\sqrt{a}+\sqrt{b}\right)^2+\left(\sqrt{a}-\sqrt{b}\right)^2}{a+b}= ....
  1. −2
  2. −1
  3. 0
  1. 1
  2. 2
SUMBER
ALTERNATIF PENYELESAIAN
\begin{aligned} \dfrac{\left(\sqrt{a}+\sqrt{b}\right)^2+\left(\sqrt{a}-\sqrt{b}\right)^2}{a+b}&=\dfrac{a+2\sqrt{ab}+b+a-2\sqrt{ab}+b}{a+b}\\ &=\dfrac{2a+2b}{a+b}\\ &=\dfrac{2(a+b)}{a+b}\\ &=\boxed{\boxed{2}} \end{aligned}
Jadi, \dfrac{\left(\sqrt{a}+\sqrt{b}\right)^2+\left(\sqrt{a}-\sqrt{b}\right)^2}{a+b}=2.
