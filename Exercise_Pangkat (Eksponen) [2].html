Jika K2x = 3, maka \(\dfrac{K^{3x}-K^{-3x}}{K^{5x}+K^{-5x}}=\)
  1. \(\dfrac{36}{122}\)
  2. \(\dfrac{39}{122}\)
  3. \(\dfrac{81}{244}\)
  1. \(\dfrac{122}{39}\)
  2. \(\dfrac{244}{81}\)
SUMBER
ALTERNATIF PENYELESAIAN
\begin{aligned} \dfrac{K^{3x}-K^{-3x}}{K^{5x}+K^{-5x}}&=\dfrac{K^{3x}-K^{-3x}}{K^{5x}+K^{-5x}}{\color{red}\cdot\dfrac{K^{5x}}{K^{5x}}}\\ &=\dfrac{K^{8x}-K^{2x}}{K^{10x}+K^0}\\ &=\dfrac{\left(K^{2x}\right)^4-K^{2x}}{\left(K^{2x}\right)^5+1}\\ &=\dfrac{3^4-3}{3^5+1}\\ &=\dfrac{81-3}{243+1}\\ &=\dfrac{78}{244}\\ &=\boxed{\boxed{\dfrac{39}{122}}} \end{aligned}
Jadi, \(\dfrac{K^{3x}-K^{-3x}}{K^{5x}+K^{-5x}}=\dfrac{39}{122}\).

Jadi, nilai $\dfrac{x^{-\frac32}\sqrt[3]{y^2}}{y^{\frac13}-x^{\frac12}}$ adalah \(\dfrac{16}{27}\).

  2. 9
  3. 18
  1. 27
  2. 36
