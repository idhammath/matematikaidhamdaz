Sederhanakan bentuk berikut: \[ \log 10^{1/2} + \log\sqrt{10^3} \]
Grup Matematika Idhamdaz
Pembahasan:

Ubah bentuk akar menjadi pangkat pecahan dan gunakan sifat $\log 10^n = n$:

\[ \begin{aligned} \log 10^{1/2} + \log\sqrt{10^3} &= \frac{1}{2} + \log 10^{3/2} \\[6pt] &= \frac{1}{2} + \frac{3}{2} \\[6pt] &= \frac{4}{2} = 2 \end{aligned} \]
Jadi, $\log 10^{1/2} + \log\sqrt{10^3} = 2$.
Sederhanakan perbandingan berikut: \[ {}^2\log\sqrt{2} : {}^2\log\sqrt{8} \]
Grup Matematika Idhamdaz
Pembahasan:

Nyatakan setiap nilai logaritma ke dalam bentuk pangkat basis $2$:

\[ \begin{aligned} {}^2\log\sqrt{2} : {}^2\log\sqrt{8} &= {}^2\log 2^{1/2} : {}^2\log \sqrt{2^3} \\[6pt] &= \frac{1}{2} : {}^2\log 2^{3/2} \\[6pt] &= \frac{1}{2} : \frac{3}{2} \\[6pt] &= \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{2}{3} = \frac{1}{3} \end{aligned} \]
Jadi, ${}^2\log\sqrt{2} : {}^2\log\sqrt{8} = \dfrac{1}{3}$.
Sederhanakan bentuk berikut: \[ {}^4\log 2 + {}^4\log 32 \]
Grup Matematika Idhamdaz
Pembahasan:

Ubah basis $4 = 2^2$ dan gunakan sifat ${}^{a^m}\log b^n = \frac{n}{m} \cdot {}^a\log b$:

\[ \begin{aligned} {}^4\log 2 + {}^4\log 32 &= {}^{2^2}\log 2 + {}^{2^2}\log 2^5 \\[6pt] &= \frac{1}{2} + \frac{5}{2} \\[6pt] &= \frac{6}{2} = 3 \end{aligned} \]

Cara alternatif: Menggunakan sifat penjumlahan logaritma:

\[ {}^4\log 2 + {}^4\log 32 = {}^4\log(2 \cdot 32) = {}^4\log 64 = {}^4\log 4^3 = 3 \]
Jadi, ${}^4\log 2 + {}^4\log 32 = 3$.

Pembahasan:

Ubah kedua bentuk logaritma ke basis $3$:

\[ \begin{aligned} {}^{45}\log 72 &= a \implies \frac{{}^3\log 72}{{}^3\log 45} = a \\ \frac{{}^3\log(3^2 \cdot 2^3)}{{}^3\log(3^2 \cdot 5)} &= a \implies \frac{2 + 3 \cdot {}^3\log 2}{2 + {}^3\log 5} = a \\ 2 + 3 \cdot {}^3\log 2 &= 2a + a \cdot {}^3\log 5 \\ 3 \cdot {}^3\log 2 - a \cdot {}^3\log 5 &= 2(a - 1) \quad \text{--- (1)} \end{aligned} \]

Untuk ${}^{20}\log 180 = b$:

\[ \begin{aligned} \frac{{}^3\log 180}{{}^3\log 20} &= b \implies \frac{{}^3\log(3^2 \cdot 2^2 \cdot 5)}{{}^3\log(2^2 \cdot 5)} = b \\ \frac{2 + 2 \cdot {}^3\log 2 + {}^3\log 5}{2 \cdot {}^3\log 2 + {}^3\log 5} &= b \\ 2 + 2 \cdot {}^3\log 2 + {}^3\log 5 &= 2b \cdot {}^3\log 2 + b \cdot {}^3\log 5 \\ 2(b - 1) \cdot {}^3\log 2 + (b - 1) \cdot {}^3\log 5 &= 2 \quad \text{--- (2)} \end{aligned} \]

Eliminasi variabel ${}^3\log 2$ dengan mengalikan persamaan (2) dengan $3$ dan persamaan (1) dengan $2(b-1)$:

\[ \begin{aligned} 6(b - 1) \cdot {}^3\log 2 + 3(b - 1) \cdot {}^3\log 5 &= 6 \\ 6(b - 1) \cdot {}^3\log 2 - 2a(b - 1) \cdot {}^3\log 5 &= 4(a - 1)(b - 1) \quad (-) \\[6pt] (3 + 2a)(b - 1) \cdot {}^3\log 5 &= 6 - 4(a - 1)(b - 1) \\[6pt] {}^3\log 5 &= \frac{6 - 4(a - 1)(b - 1)}{(2a + 3)(b - 1)} \end{aligned} \]
Jadi, ${}^3\log 5 = \dfrac{6 - 4(a - 1)(b - 1)}{(2a + 3)(b - 1)}$.

  5. $\dfrac{3b + 1}{1 + a}$
Grup Matematika Idhamdaz
Pembahasan:

Ubah bentuk eksponen menjadi bentuk logaritma basis $3$:

\[ \begin{aligned} 3^a &= 5 \implies {}^3\log 5 = a \\ 5^b &= 2 \implies {}^5\log 2 = b \\ {}^3\log 2 &= {}^3\log 5 \cdot {}^5\log 2 = ab \end{aligned} \]

Ubah basis ${}^{15}\log 40$ ke basis $3$:

\[ \begin{aligned} {}^{15}\log 40 &= \frac{{}^3\log 40}{{}^3\log 15} \\[6pt] &= \frac{{}^3\log(2^3 \cdot 5)}{{}^3\log(3 \cdot 5)} \\[6pt] &= \frac{3 \cdot {}^3\log 2 + {}^3\log 5}{{}^3\log 3 + {}^3\log 5} \\[6pt] &= \frac{3ab + a}{1 + a} \end{aligned} \]
Jadi, ${}^{15}\log 40 = \dfrac{3ab + a}{1 + a}$.

Pembahasan:

Gabungkan operasi logaritma yang sebasis:

\[ \begin{aligned} {}^5\log 9 + {}^5\log 2 - {}^5\log 450 &= {}^5\log \left(\frac{9 \cdot 2}{450}\right) \\[6pt] &= {}^5\log \left(\frac{18}{450}\right) \\[6pt] &= {}^5\log \left(\frac{1}{25}\right) \\[6pt] &= {}^5\log 5^{-2} \\ &= -2 \end{aligned} \]
Jadi, ${}^5\log 9 + {}^5\log 2 - {}^5\log 450 = -2$.

Grup Telegram
Pembahasan:

Gunakan sifat pengurangan logaritma sebasis:

\[ \begin{aligned} {}^2\log 48 - {}^2\log 6 &= {}^2\log \left(\frac{48}{6}\right) \\ &= {}^2\log 8 \\ &= {}^2\log 2^3 \\ &= 3 \end{aligned} \]
Jadi, ${}^2\log 48 - {}^2\log 6 = 3$.
