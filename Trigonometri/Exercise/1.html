Sebuah tangga disandarkan pada tembok rumah dengan membentuk sudut $60^\circ$ terhadap tanah. Jarak antara ujung tangga dan permukaan tanah adalah $2\sqrt{3}$ m. Panjang tangga tersebut adalah .....

  1. 4 m
  2. 4,5 m
  3. 5 m
  4. 5,5 m
  5. 6 m
Grup Telegram
Pembahasan:
$$\begin{aligned} \sin60^\circ&=\dfrac{2\sqrt{3}}{x}\\[3.7pt] \dfrac12\sqrt{3}&=\dfrac{2\sqrt{3}}{x}\\[3.7pt] x&=\dfrac{2\sqrt{3}}{\dfrac12\sqrt{3}}\\ &=\boxed{\boxed{4}} \end{aligned}$$
Jadi, panjang tangga tersebut adalah 4 m. (JAWAB: A)

Jika $x-y=\dfrac12\pi$ maka $\tan x$ adalah....

  1. $\dfrac{1+\tan y^2}{y}$
  2. $-\dfrac{1-y^2}{\tan y}$
  3. $\dfrac{\tan(1-y)}{(1+y)^2}$
  4. $\dfrac{\tan y}{(1+y)^2}$
  5. $-\dfrac{1}{\tan y}$
Grup WhatsApp
Pembahasan:

$x=\dfrac12\pi+y$

$$\begin{aligned} \tan x&=\tan\left(\dfrac12\pi+y\right)\\ &=-\cot y\\ &=\boxed{\boxed{-\dfrac{1}{\tan y}}} \end{aligned}$$
Jadi, $\tan x=-\dfrac{1}{\tan y}$. (JAWAB: E)

  2. $\dfrac{1-a}{\sqrt{a^2-1}}$
  3. $\dfrac{a-1}{\sqrt{1-a^2}}$
  4. $\dfrac{1-a}{\sqrt{1-a^2}}$

$$\begin{aligned} \cot257^\circ+\csc257^\circ&=\cot\left(270^\circ-13^\circ\right)+\csc\left(270^\circ-13^\circ\right)\\ &=\tan13^\circ-\sec13^\circ\\ &=\dfrac{a}{\sqrt{1-a^2}}-\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{1-a^2}}\\ &=\boxed{\boxed{\dfrac{a-1}{\sqrt{1-a^2}}}} \end{aligned}$$
Jadi, $\cot257^\circ+\csc257^\circ=\dfrac{a-1}{\sqrt{1-a^2}}$. (JAWAB: C)

Diketahui $\cos\alpha = \dfrac{a}{2b}$, dengan $\alpha$ sudut lancip dan $b \neq 0$. Nilai dari $\tan \alpha =$ ....

  1. $\dfrac{2b}{a}$
  2. $\dfrac{\sqrt{a^2-4b^2}}{2a}$
  3. $\dfrac{\sqrt{4b^2-a^2}}{2a}$
  4. $\dfrac{\sqrt{a^2-4b^2}}{a}$
  5. $\dfrac{\sqrt{4b^2-a^2}}{a}$
Grup WhatsApp
Pembahasan:

$\cos\alpha = \dfrac{a}{2b}=\dfrac{sa}{mi}$

$sa = a$, $mi = 2b$

$$\begin{aligned} de&=\sqrt{(2b)^2-a^2}\\ &=\sqrt{4b^2-a^2} \end{aligned}$$ $$\begin{aligned} \tan\alpha&=\dfrac{de}{sa}\\ &=\boxed{\boxed{\dfrac{\sqrt{4b^2-a^2}}{a}}} \end{aligned}$$
Jadi, $\tan\alpha=\dfrac{\sqrt{4b^2-a^2}}{a}$. (JAWAB: E)

Jika $\theta$ sudut lancip dan $\cos\theta=\dfrac35$, maka nilai dari $\dfrac{\sin\theta\tan\theta-1}{2\tan^2\theta}$ adalah ....

Grup WhatsApp
Pembahasan:

$\sin\theta=\dfrac45$, $\tan\theta=\dfrac43$

$$\begin{aligned} \dfrac{\sin\theta\tan\theta-1}{2\tan^2\theta}&=\dfrac{\left(\dfrac45\right)\left(\dfrac43\right)-1}{2\left(\dfrac43\right)^2}\\ &=\dfrac{\dfrac{16}{15}-1}{2\left(\dfrac{16}{9}\right)}\\ &=\dfrac{\dfrac{1}{15}}{\dfrac{32}{9}}\\ &=\dfrac{1}{15}\cdot\dfrac{9}{32}\\ &=\boxed{\boxed{\dfrac{3}{160}}} \end{aligned}$$
Jadi, nilai dari $\dfrac{\sin\theta\tan\theta-1}{2\tan^2\theta}$ adalah $\dfrac{3}{160}$.

Diketahui $\sin \alpha=\dfrac45$, $0 < \alpha < \dfrac{\pi}{2}$ dan $\cos\beta =\dfrac{12}{13}$, $-\dfrac{\pi}{2}<\beta<0$. Tentukan nilai $\sin (\alpha + \beta)$ dan $\sin (\alpha - \beta)$.

Grup Telegram
Pembahasan:

$\alpha$ kuadran I dan $\beta$ kuadran IV

$\cos\alpha=\dfrac35$, $\sin\beta=-\dfrac{5}{13}$

$$\begin{aligned} \sin (\alpha+\beta)&=\sin\alpha\cos\beta+\cos\alpha\sin\beta\\ &=\dfrac45\cdot\dfrac{12}{13}+\dfrac35\cdot\left(-\dfrac{5}{13}\right)\\ &=\dfrac{48}{65}-\dfrac{15}{65}\\ &=\boxed{\boxed{\dfrac{33}{65}}} \end{aligned}$$ $$\begin{aligned} \sin (\alpha-\beta)&=\sin\alpha\cos\beta-\cos\alpha\sin\beta\\ &=\dfrac45\cdot\dfrac{12}{13}-\dfrac35\cdot\left(-\dfrac{5}{13}\right)\\ &=\dfrac{48}{65}+\dfrac{15}{65}\\ &=\boxed{\boxed{\dfrac{63}{65}}} \end{aligned}$$
Jadi, $\sin (\alpha+\beta)=\dfrac{33}{65}$ dan $\sin (\alpha-\beta)=\dfrac{63}{65}$.

Bila $0^\circ < a < 90^\circ$ dan $\tan a=\dfrac{5}{\sqrt{11}}$, maka $\sin a =$ ....

  1. $\dfrac56$
  2. $\dfrac{25}{36}$
  3. $\dfrac16\sqrt{11}$
  4. $\dfrac{5}{36}$
  5. $\dfrac{1}{36}\sqrt{11}$
Big Book Matematika SMA
Pembahasan:
$$\begin{aligned} x&=\sqrt{\left(\sqrt{11}\right)^2+5^2}\\ &=\sqrt{11+25}\\ &=\sqrt{36}\\ &=\boxed{6} \end{aligned}$$ $$\begin{aligned} \sin a&=\dfrac{5}{x}\\ &=\boxed{\boxed{\dfrac{5}{6}}} \end{aligned}$$
Jadi, $\sin a=\dfrac56$. (JAWAB: A)
