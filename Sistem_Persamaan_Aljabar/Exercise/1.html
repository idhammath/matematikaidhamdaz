Jika \(x\) dan \(y\) memenuhi \(3^{x+1} - 3 \cdot 2^y = -3\) dan \(2 \cdot 3^x + 2^y = 10\), maka \(x + y = \dots\)
  1. 5
  2. 4
  3. 3
  4. 2
  5. 1
Matematika Zone Indonesia
Pembahasan:
Sederhanakan persamaan pertama: \[\begin{aligned} 3^{x+1} - 3 \cdot 2^y &= -3 \\ 3 \cdot 3^x - 3 \cdot 2^y &= -3 \\ 3^x - 2^y &= -1 \quad \text{...(1)} \end{aligned}\] Eliminasi dengan persamaan kedua: \[\begin{aligned} 3^x - 2^y &= -1 \\ 2 \cdot 3^x + 2^y &= 10 \quad + \\ \hline 3 \cdot 3^x &= 9 \\ 3^x &= 3 \implies x = 1 \end{aligned}\] Substitusikan \(x = 1\) ke persamaan (1): \[\begin{aligned} 3^1 - 2^y &= -1 \\ -2^y &= -4 \\ 2^y &= 4 \implies y = 2 \end{aligned}\] Sehingga nilai \(x + y\): \[x + y = 1 + 2 = 3\]
Jadi, \(x + y = 3\).

Diketahui \(x = a\) dan \(y = b\) memenuhi sistem persamaan \[\begin{cases} \dfrac{2}{x-1} + \dfrac{1}{y+1} = -6 \\[6pt] \dfrac{-2}{x-1} + \dfrac{2}{y+1} = 9 \end{cases}\] maka \(7a + 2b = \dots\)
  1. 4
  2. 5
  3. 6
  4. 7
  5. 8
Matematika Zone Indonesia
Pembahasan:
Misalkan \(p = \dfrac{1}{x-1}\) dan \(q = \dfrac{1}{y+1}\). \[\begin{aligned} 2p + q &= -6 \\ -2p + 2q &= 9 \quad + \\ \hline 3q &= 3 \implies q = 1 \end{aligned}\] Kembalikan ke variabel \(y\): \[\dfrac{1}{y+1} = 1 \implies y + 1 = 1 \implies b = y = 0\] Substitusikan \(q = 1\) ke persamaan pertama: \[\begin{aligned} 2p + 1 &= -6 \\ 2p &= -7 \implies p = -\dfrac{7}{2} \end{aligned}\] Kembalikan ke variabel \(x\): \[\begin{aligned} \dfrac{1}{x-1} &= -\dfrac{7}{2} \\ x - 1 &= -\dfrac{2}{7} \\ a = x &= 1 - \dfrac{2}{7} = \dfrac{5}{7} \end{aligned}\] Nilai \(7a + 2b\): \[7a + 2b = 7\left(\dfrac{5}{7}\right) + 2(0) = 5\]
Jadi, \(7a + 2b = 5\).

Nilai \((x - y)\) yang memenuhi sistem persamaan \(5x + 9y = 12\) dan \(\dfrac{7}{x+2} - \dfrac{3}{y+1} = 0\) adalah ....
  1. \(\dfrac{7}{2}\)
  2. \(\dfrac{9}{4}\)
  3. 2
  4. 1
  5. \(\dfrac{3}{4}\)
Grup Facebook Matematika Zone Id
Pembahasan:
Dari persamaan kedua: \[\begin{aligned} \dfrac{7}{x+2} &= \dfrac{3}{y+1} \\ 7(y + 1) &= 3(x + 2) \\ 7y + 7 &= 3x + 6 \\ -3x + 7y &= -1 \end{aligned}\] Eliminasi dengan persamaan pertama \(5x + 9y = 12\): \[\begin{aligned} 15x + 27y &= 36 \\ -15x + 35y &= -5 \quad + \\ \hline 62y &= 31 \\ y &= \dfrac{31}{62} = \dfrac{1}{2} \end{aligned}\] Substitusikan \(y = \dfrac{1}{2}\) ke \(-3x + 7y = -1\): \[\begin{aligned} -3x + 7\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right) &= -1 \\ -3x + \dfrac{7}{2} &= -1 \\ -3x &= -1 - \dfrac{7}{2} = -\dfrac{9}{2} \\ x &= \dfrac{3}{2} \end{aligned}\] Maka: \[x - y = \dfrac{3}{2} - \dfrac{1}{2} = 1\]
Jadi, \(x - y = 1\).
