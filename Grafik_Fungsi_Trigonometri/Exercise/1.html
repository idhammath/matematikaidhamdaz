ALTERNATIF PENYELESAIAN
x1 = 0, x2 = 2π, y1 = −1, y2 = 3

\(\eqalign{ a&=\dfrac{y_2-y_1}2\\ &=\dfrac{3-(-1)}2\\ &=\dfrac42\\ &=2 }\)

\(\eqalign{ b&=\dfrac{2\pi}{x_2-x_1}\\ &=\dfrac{2\pi}{2\pi-0}\\ &=1 }\)

\(\eqalign{ c&=\dfrac{y_2+y_1}2\\ &=\dfrac{3+(-1)}2\\ &=\dfrac22\\ &=1 }\)

Persamaanya,
\(\eqalign{ y&=a\cos b(x-x_1)+c\\ &=2\cos 1(x-0)+1\\ &=\boxed{\boxed{2\cos x+1}} }\)
Jadi, persamaannya adalah 2 cos x + 1.
