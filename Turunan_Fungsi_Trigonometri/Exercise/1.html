Turunan pertama dari f(x) = sin3 (3x2 − 2) adalah
  1. $2 \sin^2 (3x^2 - 2) \sin (6x^2 - 4)$
  2. $18x \sin^2 (3x^2 - 2) \cos (3x^2 - 2)$
  3. $12 \sin^2 (3x^2 - 2) \cos (6x^2 - 4)$
  4. $24 \sin^3 (3x^2 - 2) \cos^2 (3x^2 - 2)$
  5. $24 \sin^3 (3x^2 - 2) \cos (3x^2 - 2)$
Pembahasan:
\(\begin{aligned} f(x)&=\sin^3\left(3x^2-2\right)\\ f'(x)&=3\sin^2\left(3x^2-2\right)\cos\left(3x^2-2\right)\cdot 6x\\ &=\boxed{\boxed{18x\sin^2\left(3x^2-2\right)\cos\left(3x^2-2\right)}} \end{aligned}\)
Jadi, turunan pertama dari f(x) = sin3 (3x2 − 2) adalah 18x sin2 (3x2 − 2) cos (3x2 − 2).

Turunan kedua dari fungsi y = cos−1 x adalah y" adalah
  1. \(\dfrac{1-3\cos^2x}{\cos^3x}\)
  2. \(\dfrac{2+\cos^2x}{\cos^3x}\)
  3. \(\dfrac{2-\cos^2x}{\cos^3x}\)
  4. \(\dfrac{2+3\cos^2x}{\cos^3x}\)
  5. \(\dfrac{2-3\cos^2x}{\cos^3x}\)
Grup Telegram
Pembahasan:
\(\begin{aligned} y&=\cos^{-1}x\\ &=\dfrac1{\cos x}\\ &=\sec x\\ y'&=\tan x\sec x \end{aligned}\)
\(\begin{aligned} u&=\tan x\\ u'&=\sec^2x \end{aligned}\) \(\begin{aligned} v&=\sec x\\ v'&=\tan x\sec x \end{aligned}\)
\(\begin{aligned} y'&=u\cdot v\\ y'&=(\tan x)'(\sec x)+(\tan x)(\sec x)'\\ y'&=(\sec^2x)(\sec x)+(\tan x)(\tan x\sec x)\\ y'&=\sec^3x+\tan^2x\sec x\\ y'&=\sec^3x+(\sec^2x-1)\sec x\\ y'&=\sec^3x+\sec^3x-\sec x\\ y'&=2\sec^3x-\sec x\\ y'&=\dfrac{2-\cos^2x}{\cos^3x} \end{aligned}\)
Jadi, turunan kedua dari y = cos−1 x adalah \(\dfrac{2-\cos^2x}{\cos^3x}\).

Turunan kedua dari fungsi f(t) = t sin t adalah f "(t) adalah ....
  1. t sin t + 2 cos t
  2. t sin t − 2 cos t
  3. −2t sin t + cos t
  4. 2t sin t + cos t
  5. −t sin t + 2 cos t
Pembahasan:
\(\begin{aligned} u&=t\\ u'&=1 \end{aligned}\) \(\begin{aligned} v&=\sin t\\ v'&=\cos t \end{aligned}\)
\(\begin{aligned} f(t)&=uv\\ f'(t)&=u'v+uv'\\ &=1\cdot\sin t+t\cos t\\ &=\sin t+t\cos t \end{aligned}\)
\(\begin{aligned} u&=t\\ u'&=1 \end{aligned}\) \(\begin{aligned} v&=\cos t\\ v'&=-\sin t \end{aligned}\)
\(\begin{aligned} f{"}(t)&=\cos t+1\cdot\cos t+t\cdot(-\sin t)\\ &=\cos t+\cos t-t\sin t\\ &=\boxed{\boxed{-t\sin t+2\cos t}} \end{aligned}\)
Jadi, turunan kedua dari f(t) = t sin t adalah \(-t\sin t+2\cos t\).

\(\begin{aligned} y"&=-\sin x-\left(1\cdot\sin x+x\cos x\right)\\ &=-\sin x-\sin x-x\cos x\\ &=\boxed{\boxed{-x\cos x-2\sin x}} \end{aligned}\)
Jadi, y" = −x cos x − 2 sin x.

  2. −1

  4. 1
  5. 2
Grup Telegram
Pembahasan:
\(\begin{aligned} f(x)&=2\sin x+\cos x\\ f'(x)&=2\cos x-\sin x\\ f'\left(\dfrac{\pi}2\right)&=2\cos\left(\dfrac{\pi}2\right)-\sin\left(\dfrac{\pi}2\right)\\ &=2(0)-1\\ &=\boxed{\boxed{-1}} \end{aligned}\)

Diketahui f(x) = sin x cos x. Nilai turunan f(x) di titik \(x=\dfrac{\pi}6\) adalah....
  1. \(\dfrac12\sqrt3\)
  2. \(\dfrac12\sqrt2\)
  3. \(\dfrac12\)
  4. \(-\dfrac12\)
  5. \(-\dfrac12\sqrt2\)
Grup Telegram
Pembahasan:

CARA 1

\(\begin{aligned} u&=\sin x\\ u'&=\cos x \end{aligned}\) \(\begin{aligned} v&=\cos x\\ v'&=-\sin x \end{aligned}\)
\(\begin{aligned} f'(x)&=u'v+uv'\\ &=\cos x\cos x+\sin x(-\sin x)\\ &=\cos^2x-\sin^2x\\ f'\left(\dfrac{\pi}6\right)&=\cos^2\dfrac{\pi}6-\sin^2\dfrac{\pi}6\\ &=\left(\dfrac12\sqrt3\right)^2-\left(\dfrac12\right)^2\\ &=\dfrac34-\dfrac14\\ &=\dfrac24\\ &=\boxed{\boxed{\dfrac12}} \end{aligned}\)

CARA 2

\(\begin{aligned} f(x)&=\sin x\cos x\\ &=\dfrac12\cdot2\sin x\cos x\\ &=\dfrac12\sin2x\\ f'(x)&=\dfrac12\cdot2\cos2x\\ &=\cos2x\\ f'\left(\dfrac{\pi}6\right)&=\cos2\left(\dfrac{\pi}6\right)\\ &=\cos\dfrac{\pi}3\\ &=\boxed{\boxed{\dfrac12}} \end{aligned}\)
Jadi, nilai turunan f(x) di titik \(x=\dfrac{\pi}6\) adalah \(\dfrac12\).

Nilai turunan dari \(f(x)=\dfrac{\sin x+\cos x}{\cos x}\) pada \(x=\dfrac{\pi}6\) adalah ....
  1. \(\dfrac13\)
  2. \(\dfrac23\)
  3. 1
  4. \(\dfrac43\)
  5. \(\dfrac53\)
Grup Telegram
Pembahasan:

CARA 1

\(\begin{aligned} u&=\sin x+\cos x\\ u'&=\cos x-\sin x \end{aligned}\) \(\begin{aligned} v&=\cos x\\ v'&=-\sin x \end{aligned}\)
\(\begin{aligned} f(x)&=\dfrac{u}v\\ f'(x)&=\dfrac{u'v-uv'}{v^2}\\ &=\dfrac{(\cos x-\sin x)\cos x-(\sin x+\cos x)(-\sin x)}{(\cos x)^2}\\ &=\dfrac{\cos^2 x-\cancel{\sin x\cos x}+\sin^2 x+\cancel{\sin x\cos x}}{\cos^2 x}\\ &=\dfrac1{\cos^2 x}\\ f'\left(\dfrac{\pi}6\right)&=\dfrac1{\cos^2\dfrac{\pi}6}\\ &=\dfrac1{\left(\dfrac12\sqrt3\right)^2}\\ &=\dfrac1{\dfrac34}\\ &=\boxed{\boxed{\dfrac43}} \end{aligned}\)

CARA 2

\(\begin{aligned} f(x)&=\dfrac{\sin x+\cos x}{\cos x}\\ &=\dfrac{\sin x}{\cos x}+\dfrac{\cos x}{\cos x}\\ &=\tan x+1\\ f'(x)&=\sec^2x\\ f'\left(\dfrac{\pi}6\right)&=\sec^2\dfrac{\pi}6\\ &=\left(\dfrac2{\sqrt3}\right)^2\\ &=\boxed{\boxed{\dfrac43}} \end{aligned}\)
Jadi, \(f'\left(\dfrac{\pi}6\right)=\dfrac43\).

Tentukan turunan pertama fungsi-fungsi berikut.
  1. f(x) = (x + 5) sec (2x − 3)
  2. g(x) = sin 2x cos (x − 9)
Grup
Pembahasan:
  1. f(x) = (x + 5) sec (2x − 3)
    \(\begin{aligned} u&=x+5\\ u'&=1 \end{aligned}\)
    \(\begin{aligned} v&=\sec(2x-3)\\ v'&=2\tan(2x-3)\sec(2x-3) \end{aligned}\)

    \(\begin{aligned} f'(x)&=u'v+uv'\\ &=1\cdot\sec(2x-3)+(x+5)2\tan(2x-3)\sec(2x-3)\\ &=\boxed{\boxed{\sec(2x-3)+2(x+5)\tan(2x-3)\sec(2x-3)}} \end{aligned}\)

  2. g(x) = sin 2x cos (x − 9)
    \(\begin{aligned} u&=\sin2x\\ u'&=2\cos2x \end{aligned}\)
    \(\begin{aligned} v&=\cos(x-9)\\ v'&=-\sin(x-9) \end{aligned}\)

    \(\begin{aligned} g'(x)&=u'v+uv'\\ &=2\cos2x\cos(x-9)+\sin2x\left(-\sin(x-9)\right)\\ &=\boxed{\boxed{2\cos2x\cos(x-9)-\sin2x\sin(x-9)}} \end{aligned}\)
Jadi,
  1. f'(x) = sec (2x − 3) + 2(x + 5) tan (2x − 3) sec (2x − 3)
  2. g'(x) = 2 cos 2x cos (x − 9) − sin 2x sin (x − 9)
Turunan fungsi \(f(x)=2-2\sin\dfrac{\pi x}2\) bernilai nol di x1 dan x2 . Jika 0\leq x leq4 dan x1 \gt x2 , tentukan nilai x12 + x2
Grup
Pembahasan:
\(\begin{aligned} f'(x)&=0-2\cdot\dfrac{\pi}2\cos\dfrac{\pi x}2\\ &=-\pi\cos\dfrac{\pi x}2 \end{aligned}\)

\(\begin{aligned} f'(x)&=0\\ -\pi\cos\dfrac{\pi x}2&=0\\ \cos\dfrac{\pi x}2&=0\\ \cos\dfrac{\pi x}2&= \cos\dfrac{\pi}2 \end{aligned}\)
\(\begin{aligned} \dfrac{\pi x}2&=\dfrac{\pi}2+2k\pi\ &{\color{red}\times\dfrac2{\pi}}\\ x&=1+4k\\ x&=1 \end{aligned}\) \(\begin{aligned} \dfrac{\pi x}2&=-\dfrac{\pi}2+2k\pi\ &{\color{red}\times\dfrac2{\pi}}\\ x&=-1+4k\\ x&=3 \end{aligned}\)
x1 = 3, x2 = 1

\(\begin{aligned} {x_1}^2+x_2&=3^2+1\\ &=9+1\\ &=\boxed{\boxed{10}} \end{aligned}\)
Jadi, x12 + x2 = 10.
