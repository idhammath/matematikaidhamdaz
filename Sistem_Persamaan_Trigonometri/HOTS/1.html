ALTERNATIF PENYELESAIAN
\(\begin{aligned} \sin\left(x+y\right)&=1+\dfrac15\cos y\\[4pt]\sin\left(x-\right)&=-1+\cos y&{\color{red}+}\\\hline \sin\left(x+y\right)+\sin\left(x-\right)&=\dfrac65\cos y\\[4pt] 2\sin x\cos y&=\dfrac65\cos y\\[4pt] \sin x&=\dfrac35 \end{aligned}\)

\(\begin{aligned} \cos 2x&=1-2\sin^2x\\ &=1-2\left(\dfrac35\right)^2\\[4pt] &=1-2\left(\dfrac9{25}\right)\\[4pt] &=1-\dfrac{18}{25}\\ &=\color{blue}\boxed{\boxed{\color{black}\dfrac7{25}}} \end{aligned}\)
Jadi, $\cos2x=\dfrac7{25}$.
