\[\lim_{x\to0^+}\left(\cot x\right)^{\frac{1}{\ln x}}=\dots\]

Grup WhatsApp
Pembahasan:

Bentuk limit ini merupakan bentuk tak tentu \(\infty^0\). Misalkan \(y = (\cot x)^{\frac{1}{\ln x}}\), gunakan logaritma natural:

\[ \ln y = \ln\left((\cot x)^{\frac{1}{\ln x}}\right) = \dfrac{\ln(\cot x)}{\ln x} \] Ambil limit kedua ruas untuk \(x \to 0^+\): \[ \lim_{x\to0^+}\ln y = \lim_{x\to0^+}\dfrac{\ln(\cot x)}{\ln x} \] Karena berbentuk \(\dfrac{\infty}{-\infty}\), terapkan aturan L'Hôpital: \[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{x\to0^+}\dfrac{\ln(\cot x)}{\ln x} &= \lim_{x\to0^+}\dfrac{\dfrac{-\csc^2 x}{\cot x}}{\dfrac{1}{x}}\\[3.7pt] &= \lim_{x\to0^+}\dfrac{-\dfrac{1}{\sin x\cos x}}{\dfrac{1}{x}}\\[3.7pt] &= \lim_{x\to0^+}\left(-\dfrac{x}{\sin x}\cdot\dfrac{1}{\cos x}\right)\\[3.7pt] &= (-1)\cdot\dfrac{1}{1} = -1 \end{aligned} \] Maka: \[ \ln\left(\lim_{x\to0^+}y\right) = -1 \implies \lim_{x\to0^+}y = e^{-1} = \dfrac{1}{e} \]
Jadi, \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to0^+}\left(\cot x\right)^{\frac{1}{\ln x}}=\dfrac{1}{e}\).

Pembahasan:

Bagi pembilang dan penyebut dengan \((4x)^{4030}\):

\[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{\sin^{4030}2x+\tan^{4030}4x}{\sin^{4030}4x-(2x)^{4030}} &= \lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{\dfrac{\sin^{4030}2x}{(4x)^{4030}}+\dfrac{\tan^{4030}4x}{(4x)^{4030}}}{\dfrac{\sin^{4030}4x}{(4x)^{4030}}-\dfrac{(2x)^{4030}}{(4x)^{4030}}}\\[3.7pt] &= \lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{\left(\dfrac{\sin 2x}{4x}\right)^{4030}+\left(\dfrac{\tan 4x}{4x}\right)^{4030}}{\left(\dfrac{\sin 4x}{4x}\right)^{4030}-\left(\dfrac{2x}{4x}\right)^{4030}}\\[3.7pt] &= \dfrac{\left(\dfrac{2}{4}\right)^{4030}+1^{4030}}{1^{4030}-\left(\dfrac{2}{4}\right)^{4030}}\\[3.7pt] &= \dfrac{\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{4030}+1}{1-\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{4030}}\\[3.7pt] &= \dfrac{2^{4030}+1}{2^{4030}-1} = 1 + \dfrac{2}{2^{4030}-1} \end{aligned} \] Karena \(2^{4030}\) adalah bilangan yang luar biasa besar, maka \(\dfrac{2}{2^{4030}-1} \approx 0\), sehingga nilainya sangat mendekati \(1\).
Jadi, nilai yang paling mendekati adalah \(1\).

\[\lim_{x\to+\infty}\left(5^{x-1}\tan\left(\dfrac{3}{5^x}\right)\right)=\dots\]

Pembahasan:

Ubah bentuk aljabar eksponen \(5^{x-1} = \dfrac{5^x}{5}\). Misalkan \(y = 5^{-x} = \dfrac{1}{5^x}\). Saat \(x \to +\infty\), maka \(y \to 0^+\):

\[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{x\to+\infty}\left(5^{x-1}\tan\left(\dfrac{3}{5^x}\right)\right) &= \lim_{x\to+\infty}\dfrac{\tan\left(3\cdot 5^{-x}\right)}{5\cdot 5^{-x}}\\[3.7pt] &= \lim_{y\to0}\dfrac{\tan(3y)}{5y}\\[3.7pt] &= \dfrac{3}{5} \end{aligned} \]
Jadi, hasilnya adalah \(\dfrac{3}{5}\).
