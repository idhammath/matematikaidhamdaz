Hasil dari \(\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{6}+\dfrac{1}{12}+\cdots+\dfrac{1}{110}\) adalah ....
Evaluasi GMOM Oktober 2023
Pembahasan:
Gunakan identitas pecahan teleskopik: \[\dfrac{1}{n(n+1)} = \dfrac{1}{n} - \dfrac{1}{n+1}\] Maka deret dapat diuraikan sebagai berikut: \[ \begin{aligned} \dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{6}+\dfrac{1}{12}+\cdots+\dfrac{1}{110} &= \dfrac{1}{1\times2}+\dfrac{1}{2\times3}+\dfrac{1}{3\times4}+\cdots+\dfrac{1}{10\times11} \\[6pt] &= \left(\dfrac{1}{1}-\dfrac{1}{2}\right)+\left(\dfrac{1}{2}-\dfrac{1}{3}\right)+\left(\dfrac{1}{3}-\dfrac{1}{4}\right)+\cdots+\left(\dfrac{1}{10}-\dfrac{1}{11}\right) \\[6pt] &= 1 - \dfrac{1}{11} \\[6pt] &= \dfrac{10}{11} \end{aligned} \]
Jadi, hasil dari \(\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{6}+\dfrac{1}{12}+\cdots+\dfrac{1}{110} = \mathbf{\dfrac{10}{11}}\).
Misalkan \[T = \dfrac{1}{2+\sqrt{5}}+\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{5}+\sqrt{6}}+\cdots+\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2022}+\sqrt{2023}}\] maka nilai \(\lfloor T \rfloor\) adalah ....

Pembahasan:
Rasionalkan bentuk umum pecahan: \[ \begin{aligned} \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{n}+\sqrt{n+1}} &= \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{n+1}+\sqrt{n}} \times \dfrac{\sqrt{n+1}-\sqrt{n}}{\sqrt{n+1}-\sqrt{n}} \\[6pt] &= \dfrac{\sqrt{n+1}-\sqrt{n}}{(n+1)-n} \\[6pt] &= \sqrt{n+1}-\sqrt{n} \end{aligned} \] Perhatikan bahwa suku pertama: \[\dfrac{1}{2+\sqrt{5}} = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{4}+\sqrt{5}} = \sqrt{5}-\sqrt{4} = \sqrt{5}-2\] Maka jumlah \(T\) membentuk deret teleskopik: \[ \begin{aligned} T &= (\sqrt{5}-2)+(\sqrt{6}-\sqrt{5})+(\sqrt{7}-\sqrt{6})+\cdots+(\sqrt{2023}-\sqrt{2022}) \\[6pt] &= \sqrt{2023}-2 \end{aligned} \] Karena \(44^2 = 1936\) dan \(45^2 = 2025\), maka: \[44 < \sqrt{2023} < 45 \implies \sqrt{2023} \approx 44{,}97\] Sehingga: \[T \approx 44{,}97 - 2 = 42{,}97 \implies \lfloor T \rfloor = 42\]
Jadi, nilai dari \(\lfloor T \rfloor = \mathbf{42}\).
Sederhanakan bentuk penjumlahan berikut: \[\dfrac{2\times3}{1\times4}+\dfrac{5\times6}{4\times7}+\dfrac{8\times9}{7\times10}+\cdots+\dfrac{98\times99}{97\times100}\]

Pembahasan:
Bentuk umum dari suku-suku deret tersebut adalah: \[ \begin{aligned} \dfrac{(n+1)(n+2)}{n(n+3)} &= \dfrac{n^2+3n+2}{n(n+3)} \\[6pt] &= 1 + \dfrac{2}{n(n+3)} \\[6pt] &= 1 + \dfrac{2}{3}\left(\dfrac{1}{n} - \dfrac{1}{n+3}\right) \end{aligned} \] Banyaknya suku dari \(n = 1, 4, 7, \dots, 97\) adalah: \[\dfrac{97 - 1}{3} + 1 = 33\text{ suku}\] Jumlahkan seluruh suku deret: \[ \begin{aligned} \text{Jumlah} &= 33(1) + \dfrac{2}{3}\left[\left(\dfrac{1}{1}-\dfrac{1}{4}\right)+\left(\dfrac{1}{4}-\dfrac{1}{7}\right)+\cdots+\left(\dfrac{1}{97}-\dfrac{1}{100}\right)\right] \\[6pt] &= 33 + \dfrac{2}{3}\left(1 - \dfrac{1}{100}\right) \\[6pt] &= 33 + \dfrac{2}{3}\left(\dfrac{99}{100}\right) \\[6pt] &= 33 + \dfrac{33}{50} = \dfrac{1650 + 33}{50} = \dfrac{1683}{50} \end{aligned} \]
Jadi, hasil penyederhanaan deret tersebut adalah \(\dfrac{1683}{50}\).

Pembahasan:
Perhatikan penjabaran suku umum: \[ \begin{aligned} \dfrac{n}{(n+1)!} &= \dfrac{(n+1) - 1}{(n+1)!} \\[6pt] &= \dfrac{n+1}{(n+1)!} - \dfrac{1}{(n+1)!} \\[6pt] &= \dfrac{1}{n!} - \dfrac{1}{(n+1)!} \end{aligned} \] Maka penjumlahan di dalam kurung saling mengeliminasi (teleskopik): \[ \begin{aligned} \dfrac{1}{2!} + \dfrac{2}{3!} + \dfrac{3}{4!} + \cdots + \dfrac{99}{100!} &= \left(\dfrac{1}{1!} - \dfrac{1}{2!}\right) + \left(\dfrac{1}{2!} - \dfrac{1}{3!}\right) + \cdots + \left(\dfrac{1}{99!} - \dfrac{1}{100!}\right) \\[6pt] &= \dfrac{1}{1!} - \dfrac{1}{100!} = 1 - \dfrac{1}{100!} \end{aligned} \] Substitusi ke nilai \(x\): \[ \begin{aligned} x &= 101!\left(1 - \dfrac{1}{100!}\right) \\[6pt] &= 101! - \dfrac{101!}{100!} \\[6pt] &= 101! - 101 \end{aligned} \] Sehingga: \[101! - x = 101! - (101! - 101) = 101\]
Jadi, nilai dari \(101! - x = \mathbf{101}\).
Nilai dari \[\dfrac{1}{2!} + \dfrac{2}{3!} + \dfrac{3}{4!} + \cdots + \dfrac{2024}{2025!}\] dapat dinyatakan sebagai \(a - \dfrac{1}{b!}\) di mana \(a\) dan \(b\) merupakan bilangan bulat positif. Berapa nilai dari \(a + b\)?

Pembahasan:
Gunakan identitas teleskopik faktorial: \[\dfrac{n}{(n+1)!} = \dfrac{1}{n!} - \dfrac{1}{(n+1)!}\] Maka: \[ \begin{aligned} \sum_{n=1}^{2024} \dfrac{n}{(n+1)!} &= \left(\dfrac{1}{1!} - \dfrac{1}{2!}\right) + \left(\dfrac{1}{2!} - \dfrac{1}{3!}\right) + \cdots + \left(\dfrac{1}{2024!} - \dfrac{1}{2025!}\right) \\[6pt] &= \dfrac{1}{1!} - \dfrac{1}{2025!} \\[6pt] &= 1 - \dfrac{1}{2025!} \end{aligned} \] Berdasarkan bentuk \(a - \dfrac{1}{b!}\), kita peroleh: \[a = 1 \quad \text{dan} \quad b = 2025\] Maka: \[a + b = 1 + 2025 = 2026\]
Jadi, nilai dari \(a + b = \mathbf{2026}\).

Pembahasan:
Hitung nilai \(S\) menggunakan rumus deret geometri dengan \(a = \dfrac{1}{2}\), \(r = \dfrac{1}{2}\), dan \(n = 10\): \[ \begin{aligned} S &= \dfrac{\dfrac{1}{2}\left(1 - \left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{10}\right)}{1 - \dfrac{1}{2}} \\[6pt] &= 1 - \dfrac{1}{2^{10}} = 1 - \dfrac{1}{1024} = \dfrac{1023}{1024} \end{aligned} \] Kemudian hitung \(2 - S\): \[ 2 - S = 2 - \dfrac{1023}{1024} = \dfrac{2048 - 1023}{1024} = \dfrac{1025}{1024} \] Sehingga: \[ \dfrac{S}{2 - S} = \dfrac{\dfrac{1023}{1024}}{\dfrac{1025}{1024}} = \dfrac{1023}{1025} \]
Jadi, nilai dari \(\dfrac{S}{2 - S} = \mathbf{\dfrac{1023}{1025}}\).

Pembahasan:
Rasionalkan bentuk suku ke-\(n\): \[ \begin{aligned} \dfrac{1}{n\sqrt{n+1} + (n+1)\sqrt{n}} &= \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{n}\sqrt{n+1}\left(\sqrt{n+1}+\sqrt{n}\right)} \times \dfrac{\sqrt{n+1}-\sqrt{n}}{\sqrt{n+1}-\sqrt{n}} \\[6pt] &= \dfrac{\sqrt{n+1}-\sqrt{n}}{\sqrt{n}\sqrt{n+1}((n+1)-n)} \\[6pt] &= \dfrac{\sqrt{n+1}-\sqrt{n}}{\sqrt{n}\sqrt{n+1}} \\[6pt] &= \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{n}} - \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{n+1}} \end{aligned} \] Dengan menerapkan bentuk teleskopik dari \(n = 1\) hingga \(n = 2014^2 - 1\): \[ \begin{aligned} \text{Jumlah} &= \left(\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{1}}-\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\right) + \left(\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}-\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\right) + \cdots + \left(\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2014^2-1}}-\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2014^2}}\right) \\[6pt] &= \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{1}} - \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2014^2}} \\[6pt] &= 1 - \dfrac{1}{2014} \\[6pt] &= \dfrac{2013}{2014} \end{aligned} \]
Jadi, hasil dari penjumlahan tersebut adalah \(\dfrac{2013}{2014}\).
