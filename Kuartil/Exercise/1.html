Q1

Letak Q1

\dfrac{n}4=\dfrac{40}4=10
Kelas yang nilai Fk-nya lebih dari 10 dimulai dari kelas ke-3, sehingga Q1 terletak di kelas ke-3.
Tb = 129,5
Fk = 7 (nilai Fk sebelum kelas Q1)
F = 10
p = 5 \begin{aligned} Q_1&=T_b+\left(\dfrac{\dfrac{n}4-F_k}F\right)p\\ &=129{,}5+\left(\dfrac{10-7}{10}\right)5\\ &=129{,}5+\left(\dfrac3{10}\right)5\\ &=129{,}5+\dfrac{15}{10}\\ &=129{,}5+1{,}5\\ &=\boxed{\boxed{131}} \end{aligned}

Q2

Tb = 134,5
Fk = 17 (nilai Fk sebelum kelas Q2)
F = 7
p = 5 \begin{aligned} Q_2&=T_b+\left(\dfrac{\dfrac{n}2-F_k}F\right)p\\ &=134{,}5+\left(\dfrac{20-17}7\right)5\\ &=134{,}5+\left(\dfrac37\right)5\\ &=134{,}5+\dfrac{15}7\\ &=134{,}5+2{,}14\\ &=\boxed{\boxed{136{,}64}} \end{aligned}

Q3

Letak Q3

\dfrac{3n}3=\dfrac{3\cdot40}4=30

p = 5 \begin{aligned} Q_3&=T_b+\left(\dfrac{\dfrac{3n}4-F_k}F\right)p\\ &=139{,}5+\left(\dfrac{30-24}{11}\right)5\\ &=139{,}5+\left(\dfrac6{11}\right)5\\ &=139{,}5+\dfrac{30}{11}\\ &=139{,}5+2{,}73\\ &=\boxed{\boxed{142{,}23}} \end{aligned}
Jadi, Q1 = 136,64, Q2 = 136,64, Q3 = 142,23.
