Berikut adalah soal tingkat ... materi Limit Tak Hingga

Nilai dari: \[\lim_{x\to\infty}\left(\sqrt{x^2+4x+1}-\sqrt{x^2-2x+3}\right)=\dots\]

  1. \(1\)
  2. \(2\)
  3. \(3\)
  4. \(4\)
  5. \(6\)
Matematika Idhamdaz
Pembahasan:

Cara 1: Mengalikan dengan Bentuk Sekawan

\[ \begin{aligned} &\lim_{x\to\infty}\left(\sqrt{x^2+4x+1}-\sqrt{x^2-2x+3}\right)\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt{x^2+4x+1}+\sqrt{x^2-2x+3}}{\sqrt{x^2+4x+1}+\sqrt{x^2-2x+3}}\\[3.7pt] &=\lim_{x\to\infty}\dfrac{(x^2+4x+1)-(x^2-2x+3)}{\sqrt{x^2+4x+1}+\sqrt{x^2-2x+3}}\\[3.7pt] &=\lim_{x\to\infty}\dfrac{6x-2}{\sqrt{x^2+4x+1}+\sqrt{x^2-2x+3}}\\[3.7pt] &=\lim_{x\to\infty}\dfrac{6-\dfrac{2}{x}}{\sqrt{1+\dfrac{4}{x}+\dfrac{1}{x^2}}+\sqrt{1-\dfrac{2}{x}+\dfrac{3}{x^2}}}\\[3.7pt] &=\dfrac{6-0}{\sqrt{1}+\sqrt{1}} = \dfrac{6}{2} = 3 \end{aligned} \]

Cara 2: Rumus Cepat \(\dfrac{b-q}{2\sqrt{a}}\)

Bentuk \(\lim_{x\to\infty}\left(\sqrt{ax^2+bx+c}-\sqrt{ax^2+qx+r}\right)\) dengan \(a = 1\), \(b = 4\), dan \(q = -2\):

\[ \dfrac{b-q}{2\sqrt{a}} = \dfrac{4-(-2)}{2\sqrt{1}} = \dfrac{6}{2} = 3 \]
Jadi, \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to\infty}\left(\sqrt{x^2+4x+1}-\sqrt{x^2-2x+3}\right)=3\).
