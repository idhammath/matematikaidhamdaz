ALTERNATIF PENYELESAIAN
Titik tengah A dan A'
$B\left(\dfrac{1+3}2,\dfrac{-2+4}2\right)=B(2,1)$

B dilalui oleh garis ax + by + 2 = 0
$\begin{aligned} 2a+b+2&=0\\ 2a+b&=-2 \end{aligned}$

Gradien garis AA'
$\begin{aligned} m_{AA'}&=\dfrac{y_{A'}-y_A}{x_{A'}-x_A}\\ &=\dfrac{4-(-2)}{3-1}\\ &=\dfrac62\\ &=3 \end{aligned}$

gradien garis ax + by + 2 = 0
$\begin{aligned} m&=-\dfrac1{m_{AA'}}\\ -\dfrac{a}b&=-\dfrac13\\ \dfrac{a}b&=\dfrac13\\ b&=3a \end{aligned}$

$\begin{aligned} 2a+b&=-2\\ 2a+3a&=-2\\ 5a&=-2\\ a&=-\dfrac25 \end{aligned}$

$\begin{aligned} a+b&=a+3a\\ &=4a\\ &=4\left(-\dfrac25\right)\\ &=\boxed{\boxed{-\dfrac85}} \end{aligned}$
Jadi, ${a + b=-\dfrac85}$.
