Tentukan nilai dari: \[ \log 25 \times {}^2\log 10 \times {}^5\log 4 \]
Grup Telegram
Pembahasan:

Susun urutan perkalian dan gunakan sifat rantai perkalian logaritma:

\[ \begin{aligned} \log 25 \times {}^2\log 10 \times {}^5\log 4 &= \log 25 \times {}^5\log 4 \times {}^2\log 10 \\[6pt] &= \log 25 \times {}^5\log 2^2 \times {}^2\log 10 \\[6pt] &= 2 \times \log 25 \times ({}^5\log 2 \times {}^2\log 10) \\[6pt] &= 2 \times \log 25 \times {}^5\log 10 \\[6pt] &= 2 \times \frac{\log 25}{\log 5} \\[6pt] &= 2 \times {}^5\log 25 \\[6pt] &= 2 \times 2 = 4 \end{aligned} \]
Jadi, $\log 25 \times {}^2\log 10 \times {}^5\log 4 = 4$.
Jika ${}^2\log 3 = a$ dan ${}^2\log 5 = b$, nyatakan ${}^6\log 50$ dalam bentuk $a$ dan $b$.
Grup Telegram
Pembahasan:

Ubah basis ${}^6\log 50$ ke basis $2$:

\[ \begin{aligned} {}^6\log 50 &= \frac{{}^2\log 50}{{}^2\log 6} \\[6pt] &= \frac{{}^2\log(5^2 \cdot 2)}{{}^2\log(3 \cdot 2)} \\[6pt] &= \frac{{}^2\log 5^2 + {}^2\log 2}{{}^2\log 3 + {}^2\log 2} \\[6pt] &= \frac{2 \cdot {}^2\log 5 + 1}{{}^2\log 3 + 1} \\[6pt] &= \frac{2b + 1}{a + 1} \end{aligned} \]
Jadi, ${}^6\log 50 = \dfrac{2b + 1}{a + 1}$.
Hitunglah nilai dari: \[ {}^2\log 1 + {}^3\log 3 - {}^5\log 2 + {}^5\log 50 - {}^8\log 32 = \dots \]
Grup Telegram
Pembahasan:

Hitung nilai masing-masing suku:

\[ \begin{aligned} {}^2\log 1 &= 0 \\ {}^3\log 3 &= 1 \\ {}^5\log 50 - {}^5\log 2 &= {}^5\log\left(\frac{50}{2}\right) = {}^5\log 25 = 2 \\ {}^8\log 32 &= {}^{2^3}\log 2^5 = \frac{5}{3} \end{aligned} \]

Gabungkan seluruh suku:

\[ 0 + 1 + 2 - \frac{5}{3} = 3 - \frac{5}{3} = \frac{9 - 5}{3} = \frac{4}{3} \]
Jadi, hasil perhitungannya adalah $\dfrac{4}{3}$.
Hitunglah nilai dari: \[ {}^5\log 35 + {}^5\log 55 - {}^5\log 77 = \dots \]
Grup Telegram
Pembahasan:

Gunakan sifat operasi penjumlahan dan pengurangan logaritma sebasis:

\[ \begin{aligned} {}^5\log 35 + {}^5\log 55 - {}^5\log 77 &= {}^5\log\left(\frac{35 \cdot 55}{77}\right) \\[6pt] &= {}^5\log\left(\frac{(5 \cdot 7) \cdot (5 \cdot 11)}{7 \cdot 11}\right) \\[6pt] &= {}^5\log(5 \cdot 5) \\[6pt] &= {}^5\log 25 = 2 \end{aligned} \]
Jadi, ${}^5\log 35 + {}^5\log 55 - {}^5\log 77 = 2$.
Jika $\dfrac{a^2}{b^2} = 4$ untuk bilangan positif $a$ dan $b$, maka tentukan nilai dari: \[ \log \frac{a^3}{b^3} = \dots \]
Grup Telegram
Pembahasan:

Dari persamaan $\frac{a^2}{b^2} = 4$:

\[ \left(\frac{a}{b}\right)^2 = 4 \implies \frac{a}{b} = 2 \]

Substitusikan ke bentuk yang ditanyakan:

\[ \begin{aligned} \log \frac{a^3}{b^3} &= \log \left(\frac{a}{b}\right)^3 \\ &= \log 2^3 \\ &= \log 8 \end{aligned} \]
Jadi, $\log \dfrac{a^3}{b^3} = \log 8$ (atau $3\log 2$).
Sederhanakan bentuk berikut: \[ {}^3\log \frac{1}{3} \]
Grup Telegram
Pembahasan:

Nyatakan $\frac{1}{3}$ dalam pangkat negatif basis $3$:

\[ {}^3\log \frac{1}{3} = {}^3\log 3^{-1} = -1 \cdot {}^3\log 3 = -1 \]
Jadi, ${}^3\log \dfrac{1}{3} = -1$.
Jika ${}^2\log 5 = m$ dan ${}^2\log 3 = n$, nyatakan ${}^{50}\log 90$ dalam bentuk $m$ dan $n$.
Grup Telegram
Pembahasan:

Ubah basis ${}^{50}\log 90$ ke basis $2$:

\[ \begin{aligned} {}^{50}\log 90 &= \frac{{}^2\log 90}{{}^2\log 50} \\[6pt] &= \frac{{}^2\log(5 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 2)}{{}^2\log(5^2 \cdot 2)} \\[6pt] &= \frac{{}^2\log 5 + 2 \cdot {}^2\log 3 + {}^2\log 2}{2 \cdot {}^2\log 5 + {}^2\log 2} \\[6pt] &= \frac{m + 2n + 1}{2m + 1} \end{aligned} \]
Jadi, ${}^{50}\log 90 = \dfrac{m + 2n + 1}{2m + 1}$.

\[ \begin{aligned} {}^3\log 36 \cdot {}^6\log 81 &= {}^3\log 6^2 \cdot {}^6\log 81 = 2 \cdot ({}^3\log 6 \cdot {}^6\log 81) \\ &= 2 \cdot {}^3\log 81 = 2 \cdot 4 = 8 \\[6pt] {}^4\log 32 &= {}^{2^2}\log 2^5 = \frac{5}{2} \end{aligned} \]

Hasil dari: \[ \left({}^{c^{7/2}}\log b^{14}\right) \cdot \left({}^{a^{5/3}}\log c^{10}\right) \cdot \left({}^{b^{3/2}}\log a^9\right) \] adalah ...

  2. $155$
  3. $166$
  4. $177$
  5. $188$
SKMP Juli 2020
Pembahasan:

Gunakan sifat ${}^{x^m}\log y^n = \frac{n}{m} \cdot {}^x\log y$ untuk masing-masing faktor:

\[ \begin{aligned} {}^{c^{7/2}}\log b^{14} &= \frac{14}{\frac{7}{2}} \cdot {}^c\log b = 14 \cdot \frac{2}{7} \cdot {}^c\log b = 4 \cdot {}^c\log b \\[6pt] {}^{a^{5/3}}\log c^{10} &= \frac{10}{\frac{5}{3}} \cdot {}^a\log c = 10 \cdot \frac{3}{5} \cdot {}^a\log c = 6 \cdot {}^a\log c \\[6pt] {}^{b^{3/2}}\log a^9 &= \frac{9}{\frac{3}{2}} \cdot {}^b\log a = 9 \cdot \frac{2}{3} \cdot {}^b\log a = 6 \cdot {}^b\log a \end{aligned} \]

Kalikan ketiga hasil tersebut:

\[ \begin{aligned} (4 \cdot {}^c\log b) \cdot (6 \cdot {}^a\log c) \cdot (6 \cdot {}^b\log a) &= (4 \cdot 6 \cdot 6) \cdot ({}^a\log c \cdot {}^c\log b \cdot {}^b\log a) \\[6pt] &= 144 \cdot {}^a\log a \\[6pt] &= 144 \cdot 1 = 144 \end{aligned} \]
Jadi, hasil perkalian tersebut adalah $144$.
