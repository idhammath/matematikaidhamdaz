No. 11
Nilai dari \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to\frac{\pi}{2}}\frac{\sin\left(x-\frac{\pi}{2}\right)}{\sqrt{\frac{x}{2}}-\sqrt{\frac{\pi}{4}}}\) adalah ...
  1. \(4\sqrt{\pi}\)
  2. \(2\sqrt{\pi}\)
  3. \(\sqrt{\pi}\)
  4. \(\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{\pi}\)
  5. \(\frac{1}{4}\sqrt{\pi}\)
Pembahasan & Solusi:

Kalikan pembilang dan penyebut dengan bentuk sekawan dari penyebut, yaitu \(\left(\sqrt{\frac{x}{2}}+\sqrt{\frac{\pi}{4}}\right)\):

\[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{x\to\frac{\pi}{2}}\frac{\sin\left(x-\frac{\pi}{2}\right)}{\sqrt{\frac{x}{2}}-\sqrt{\frac{\pi}{4}}} &= \lim_{x\to\frac{\pi}{2}}\frac{\sin\left(x-\frac{\pi}{2}\right)\left(\sqrt{\frac{x}{2}}+\sqrt{\frac{\pi}{4}}\right)}{\frac{x}{2}-\frac{\pi}{4}} \\[6pt] &= \lim_{x\to\frac{\pi}{2}}\frac{\left(\sqrt{\frac{x}{2}}+\sqrt{\frac{\pi}{4}}\right)\sin\left(x-\frac{\pi}{2}\right)}{\frac{1}{2}\left(x-\frac{\pi}{2}\right)} \\[6pt] &= \lim_{x\to\frac{\pi}{2}} 2\left(\sqrt{\frac{x}{2}}+\sqrt{\frac{\pi}{4}}\right) \cdot \frac{\sin\left(x-\frac{\pi}{2}\right)}{x-\frac{\pi}{2}} \end{aligned} \]

Gunakan sifat \(\displaystyle\lim_{u\to 0}\frac{\sin u}{u} = 1\) dengan \(u = x - \frac{\pi}{2}\):

\[ \begin{aligned} &= 2\left(\sqrt{\frac{\pi/2}{2}}+\sqrt{\frac{\pi}{4}}\right) \cdot 1 \\[6pt] &= 2\left(\sqrt{\frac{\pi}{4}}+\sqrt{\frac{\pi}{4}}\right) \\[6pt] &= 2\left(\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{\pi}+\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{\pi}\right) \\[6pt] &= 2\sqrt{\pi} \end{aligned} \]
Kesimpulan: Nilai limit tersebut adalah \(2\sqrt{\pi}\).
Jawaban: B
No. 12 SKMP September 2020
\(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to0}\frac{3x}{\left(1-\sqrt{\cos 2x}\right)\csc 2x} = \dots\)
  1. 8
  2. 6
  3. 4
  4. 3
  5. 2
Pembahasan & Solusi:

Ingat bahwa \(\csc 2x = \frac{1}{\sin 2x}\), sehingga pecahan dapat ditulis ulang menjadi:

\[ \lim_{x\to0}\frac{3x\sin 2x}{1-\sqrt{\cos 2x}} \]

Rasionalkan penyebut dengan mengalikan sekawan \(\left(1+\sqrt{\cos 2x}\right)\):

\[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{x\to0}\frac{3x\sin 2x}{1-\sqrt{\cos 2x}} \cdot \frac{1+\sqrt{\cos 2x}}{1+\sqrt{\cos 2x}} &= \lim_{x\to0}\frac{3x\sin 2x\left(1+\sqrt{\cos 2x}\right)}{1-\cos 2x} \end{aligned} \]

Gunakan rumus sudut rangkap \(\cos 2x = 1 - 2\sin^2 x \iff 1 - \cos 2x = 2\sin^2 x\):

\[ \begin{aligned} &= \lim_{x\to0}\frac{3x\sin 2x\left(1+\sqrt{\cos 2x}\right)}{2\sin^2 x} \\[6pt] &= \lim_{x\to0}\frac{3}{2} \cdot \frac{x}{\sin x} \cdot \frac{\sin 2x}{\sin x} \cdot \left(1+\sqrt{\cos 2x}\right) \\[6pt] &= \frac{3}{2} \cdot 1 \cdot \frac{2}{1} \cdot \left(1+\sqrt{\cos 0}\right) \\[6pt] &= 3 \cdot (1 + 1) \\[6pt] &= 6 \end{aligned} \]
Kesimpulan: Nilai dari limit tersebut adalah \(6\).
Jawaban: B
No. 13 SKMP Juli 2020
\(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to0}\sqrt{\frac{x\tan x}{1+\sin^2 x-\cos 2x}} = \dots\)
  1. \(\frac{1}{3}\)
  2. \(\frac{1}{3}\sqrt{3}\)
  3. \(\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{3}\)
  4. \(\sqrt{3}\)
  5. \(3\)
Pembahasan & Solusi:

Ubah bentuk penyebut menggunakan identitas trigonometri \(\cos 2x = 1 - 2\sin^2 x\):

\[ 1 + \sin^2 x - \cos 2x = 1 + \sin^2 x - (1 - 2\sin^2 x) = 3\sin^2 x \]

Substitusikan ke dalam bentuk limit:

\[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{x\to0}\sqrt{\frac{x\tan x}{1+\sin^2 x-\cos 2x}} &= \lim_{x\to0}\sqrt{\frac{x\tan x}{3\sin^2 x}} \\[6pt] &= \lim_{x\to0}\sqrt{\frac{1}{3} \cdot \frac{x}{\sin x} \cdot \frac{\tan x}{\sin x}} \\[6pt] &= \sqrt{\frac{1}{3} \cdot 1 \cdot 1} \\[6pt] &= \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} = \frac{1}{3}\sqrt{3} \end{aligned} \]
Kesimpulan: Nilai dari limit tersebut adalah \(\frac{1}{3}\sqrt{3}\).
Jawaban: B
No. 14 SKMP September 2020
\(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to0}\frac{3x}{\left(1-\sqrt{\cos 2x}\right)\csc 2x} = \dots\)
  1. 8
  2. 6
  3. 4
  4. 3
  5. 2
Pembahasan & Solusi (Metode Alternatif):

Selain menggunakan sudut ganda sinus, kita juga dapat mengalikan berturut-turut dengan bentuk sekawan akar dan sekawan kosinus:

\[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{x\to0}\frac{3x\sin 2x}{1-\sqrt{\cos 2x}} \cdot \frac{1+\sqrt{\cos 2x}}{1+\sqrt{\cos 2x}} &= \lim_{x\to0}\frac{3x\sin 2x\left(1+\sqrt{\cos 2x}\right)}{1-\cos 2x} \cdot \frac{1+\cos 2x}{1+\cos 2x} \\[6pt] &= \lim_{x\to0}\frac{3x\sin 2x\left(1+\sqrt{\cos 2x}\right)(1+\cos 2x)}{1-\cos^2 2x} \\[6pt] &= \lim_{x\to0}\frac{3x\sin 2x\left(1+\sqrt{\cos 2x}\right)(1+\cos 2x)}{\sin^2 2x} \\[6pt] &= \lim_{x\to0}\frac{3x}{\sin 2x}\cdot\frac{\sin 2x}{\sin 2x}\cdot\left(1+\sqrt{\cos 2x}\right)(1+\cos 2x) \\[6pt] &= \frac{3}{2}\cdot 1 \cdot \left(1+\sqrt{\cos 0}\right)(1+\cos 0) \\[6pt] &= \frac{3}{2}\cdot (1+1)\cdot (1+1) \\[6pt] &= \frac{3}{2}\cdot 2 \cdot 2 = 6 \end{aligned} \]
Kesimpulan: Nilai limit tersebut adalah \(6\).
Jawaban: B
No. 15 SIMAK UI 2011
\(\displaystyle\lim_{a\to b}\frac{\tan a-\tan b}{1+\left(1-\frac{a}{b}\right)\tan a\tan b-\frac{a}{b}} = \dots\)
  1. \(\frac{1}{b}\)
  2. \(b\)
  3. \(-b\)
  4. \(-\frac{1}{b}\)
  5. 1
Pembahasan & Solusi:

Kelompokkan dan faktorkan bagian penyebut pecahan:

\[ \begin{aligned} 1+\left(1-\frac{a}{b}\right)\tan a\tan b-\frac{a}{b} &= 1 - \frac{a}{b} + \left(1-\frac{a}{b}\right)\tan a\tan b \\ &= \left(1 - \frac{a}{b}\right)(1 + \tan a\tan b) \end{aligned} \]

Ingat identitas pengurangan tangen \(\frac{\tan a - \tan b}{1 + \tan a\tan b} = \tan(a - b)\):

\[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{a\to b}\frac{\tan a-\tan b}{\left(1-\frac{a}{b}\right)(1+\tan a\tan b)} &= \lim_{a\to b}\frac{\tan(a - b)}{1 - \frac{a}{b}} \\[6pt] &= \lim_{a\to b}\frac{\tan(a - b)}{\frac{b - a}{b}} \\[6pt] &= \lim_{a\to b}\frac{\tan(a - b)}{\frac{a - b}{-b}} \\[6pt] &= \lim_{a\to b}\frac{-b\tan(a - b)}{a - b} \end{aligned} \]

Misalkan \(x = a - b\). Jika \(a \to b\), maka \(x \to 0\):

\[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{x\to0}\frac{-b\tan x}{x} &= -b \lim_{x\to0}\frac{\tan x}{x} \\[6pt] &= -b \cdot 1 = -b \end{aligned} \]
Kesimpulan: Nilai dari limit tersebut adalah \(-b\).
Jawaban: C
