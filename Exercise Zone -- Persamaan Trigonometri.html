ALTERNATIF PENYELESAIAN
\(\begin{aligned} \sin x&=2\sin2x\\ \sin x&=2(2\sin x\cos x)\\ \sin x&=4\sin x\cos x\\ 1&=4\cos x\\ \cos x&=\dfrac14 \end{aligned}\)

\(\begin{aligned} \cos2x&=2\cos^2x-1\\ &=2\left(\dfrac14\right)^2-1\\ &=2\left(\dfrac1{16}\right)-1\\ &=\dfrac2{16}-1\\ &=\boxed{\boxed{-\dfrac{14}{16}}} \end{aligned}\)
Jadi, \(\cos 2x=-\dfrac{14}{16}\).
