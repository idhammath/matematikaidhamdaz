\(\sqrt{5050^2-4950^2}=\) ....
  1. 10
  2. 100
  3. 1000
  1. 10000
  2. 100000
OSK SMP 2004
ALTERNATIF PENYELESAIAN
\begin{aligned} \sqrt{5050^2-4950^2}&=\sqrt{(5050+4950)(5050-4950)}\\ &=\sqrt{(10000)(100)}\\ &=\sqrt{1000000}\\ &=\boxed{\boxed{1000}} \end{aligned}
Jadi, \(\sqrt{5050^2-4950^2}=1000\).

Nilai dari \(\sqrt{1+68\cdot69\cdot70\cdot71}=\) ....
Forum Matematika
ALTERNATIF PENYELESAIAN
Misal x = 70. \begin{aligned} \sqrt{1+68\cdot69\cdot70\cdot71}&=\sqrt{1+(x-2)(x-1)x(x+1)}\\ &=\sqrt{1+\left((x-2)(x+1)\right)\left(x(x-1)\right)}\\ &=\sqrt{1+\left(x^2-x-2\right)\left(x^2-x\right)} \end{aligned} Misal p = x2 − x \begin{aligned} \sqrt{1+68\cdot69\cdot70\cdot71}&=\sqrt{1+(p-2)p}\\ &=\sqrt{1+p^2-2p}\\ &=\sqrt{(p-1)^2}\\ &=p-1\\ &=x^2-x-1\\ &=70^2-70-1\\ &=\boxed{\boxed{4829}} \end{aligned}
Jadi, \(\sqrt{1+68\cdot69\cdot70\cdot71}=4829\).

Selisih nilai x terbesar dan terkecil yang memenuhi persamaan $\sqrt{x-1}+\sqrt{x+1}=\sqrt{3x}$ adalah ....
  1. 2
  2. $\dfrac23\sqrt3$
  1. $\sqrt3$
  2. $\dfrac43\sqrt3$
  1. $2\sqrt3$
FSN 2023
ALTERNATIF PENYELESAIAN
\(\begin{aligned} \sqrt{x-1}+\sqrt{x+1}&=\sqrt{3x}\\ x-1+2\sqrt{x^2-1}+x+1&=3x\\ 2\sqrt{x^2-1}&=x\\ 4\left(x^2-1\right)&=x^2\\ 3x^2-4&=0\\ x^2&=\dfrac43\\[4pt] x&=\pm\sqrt{\dfrac43}\\ &=\pm\dfrac23\sqrt3 \end{aligned}\)

$\dfrac23\sqrt3-\left(-\dfrac23\sqrt3\right)=\color{blue}\boxed{\boxed{\color{black}\dfrac43\sqrt3}}$
Jadi, Selisih nilai x terbesar dan terkecil yang memenuhi persamaan $\sqrt{x-1}+\sqrt{x+1}=\sqrt{3x}$ adalah $\dfrac43\sqrt3$.

Diketahui bahwa selisih bentuk $$\sqrt{57-40\sqrt2}-\sqrt{57+40\sqrt2}$$ merupakan bilangan bulat, tentukan bilangan bulat tersebut.
Grup WhatsApp
ALTERNATIF PENYELESAIAN
\(\begin{aligned} \sqrt{57-40\sqrt2}-\sqrt{57+40\sqrt2}&=\sqrt{57-2\sqrt{800}}-\sqrt{57+2\sqrt{800}}\\ &=\sqrt{32+25-2\sqrt{32\cdot25}}-\sqrt{32+25+2\sqrt{32\cdot25}}\\ &=\left(\sqrt{32}-\sqrt{25}\right)-\left(\sqrt{32}+\sqrt{25}\right)\\ &=\sqrt{32}-5-\sqrt{32}-5\\ &=\color{blue}\boxed{\boxed{\color{black}-10}} \end{aligned}\)
Jadi, bilangan bulat tersebut adalah −10.
