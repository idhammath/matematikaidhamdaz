ALTERNATIF PENYELESAIAN
\(\begin{aligned} 10\times21+10\times22+\cdots+10\times30+11\times21+\cdots+11\times30+\cdots+20\times30&=10\times(21+22+\cdots+30)+11\times(21+22+\cdots+30)+\cdots+20\times(21+22+\cdots+30)\\ &=(10+11+\cdots+20)\times(21+22+\cdots+30)\\ &=\left(\dfrac{11(10+20)}2\right)\times\left(\dfrac{10(21+30)}2\right)\\[4pt] &=165\times255\\ &=\boxed{\boxed{42075}} \end{aligned}\)
Jadi, jumlah semua hasil kalinya adalah 42.705.

ALTERNATIF PENYELESAIAN
Banyak suku dari $\left(3+\dfrac1{2020}\right)$ sampai $\left(3+\dfrac{4039}{2020}\right)$
$n=\dfrac{4039-1}{2}+1=2020$

\(\begin{aligned} 2025+\left(3+\dfrac1{2020}\right)+\left(3+\dfrac3{2020}\right)+\left(3+\dfrac5{2020}\right)+\cdots+\left(3+\dfrac{4039}{2020}\right)&=2025+2020\times3+\dfrac{1+3+5+\cdots+4039}{2020}\\[4pt] &=2025+6060+\dfrac{\dfrac{2020}2(1+4039)}{2020}\\[4pt] &=8085+2020\\ &=\boxed{\boxed{10105}} \end{aligned}\)
Jadi, hasil dari operasi $2025+\left(3+\dfrac1{2020}\right)+\left(3+\dfrac3{2020}\right)+\left(3+\dfrac5{2020}\right)+\cdots+\left(3+\dfrac{4039}{2020}\right)=10{.}105$.
