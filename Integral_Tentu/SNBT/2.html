Hasil dari \(\displaystyle\int_{10}^{12} \dfrac{dx}{\sqrt{28x + 8x\sqrt{x}}} = \dots\)

  1. \(\sqrt{2} + \sqrt{3} - 2 + \sqrt{5}\)
  2. \(\sqrt{2} + \sqrt{3} + 2 - \sqrt{5}\)
  3. \(-\sqrt{2} + \sqrt{3} + 2 - \sqrt{5}\)
  4. \(-\sqrt{2} + \sqrt{3} - 2 + \sqrt{5}\)
  5. \(-\sqrt{2} + \sqrt{3} + 2 + \sqrt{5}\)
Facebook
Pembahasan:

Faktorkan penyebut di dalam akar:

\[\begin{aligned} \sqrt{28x + 8x\sqrt{x}} &= \sqrt{4x(7 + 2\sqrt{x})} \\ &= 2\sqrt{x}\sqrt{7 + 2\sqrt{x}} \end{aligned}\]

Gunakan teknik substitusi:

Misalkan: \[u = 7 + 2\sqrt{x} \implies du = 2 \cdot \dfrac{1}{2\sqrt{x}} \, dx = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{x}} \, dx\] Sehingga: \[\dfrac{dx}{2\sqrt{x}\sqrt{7 + 2\sqrt{x}}} = \dfrac{du}{2\sqrt{u}} = \dfrac{1}{2} u^{-\frac{1}{2}} \, du\]

Integralkan:

\[\int \dfrac{1}{2} u^{-\frac{1}{2}} \, du = u^{\frac{1}{2}} + C = \sqrt{7 + 2\sqrt{x}} + C\]

Evaluasi pada batas \(x = 10\) sampai \(x = 12\):

\[\begin{aligned} \left[\sqrt{7 + 2\sqrt{x}}\right]_{10}^{12} &= \sqrt{7 + 2\sqrt{12}} - \sqrt{7 + 2\sqrt{10}} \\ &= \sqrt{4 + 3 + 2\sqrt{4 \cdot 3}} - \sqrt{2 + 5 + 2\sqrt{2 \cdot 5}} \\ &= (\sqrt{4} + \sqrt{3}) - (\sqrt{2} + \sqrt{5}) \\ &= 2 + \sqrt{3} - \sqrt{2} - \sqrt{5} \\ &= -\sqrt{2} + \sqrt{3} + 2 - \sqrt{5} \end{aligned}\]
Jadi, hasil integral tersebut adalah \(-\sqrt{2} + \sqrt{3} + 2 - \sqrt{5}\).

Pembahasan:

Karena \(f(x)\) genap dan \(\sin x\) ganjil, maka \(f(x)\sin x\) merupakan fungsi ganjil sehingga \(\displaystyle\int_{-3}^3 f(x)\sin x \, dx = 0\).

\[\begin{aligned} \int_{-3}^3 f(x)(\sin x + 1) \, dx &= \int_{-3}^3 f(x)\sin x \, dx + \int_{-3}^3 f(x) \, dx \\ 10 &= 0 + 2\int_0^3 f(x) \, dx \\ \int_0^3 f(x) \, dx &= 5 \end{aligned}\]

Karena \(f(x)\) simetris terhadap sumbu-\(y\) (fungsi genap), maka \(\displaystyle\int_{-1}^0 f(x) \, dx = \int_0^1 f(x) \, dx\):

\[\begin{aligned} \int_{-1}^3 f(x) \, dx &= \int_{-1}^0 f(x) \, dx + \int_0^3 f(x) \, dx \\ 7 &= \int_{-1}^0 f(x) \, dx + 5 \\ \int_{-1}^0 f(x) \, dx &= 2 \implies \int_0^1 f(x) \, dx = 2 \end{aligned}\]

Maka nilai \(\displaystyle\int_1^3 f(x) \, dx\) adalah:

\[\begin{aligned} \int_1^3 f(x) \, dx &= \int_0^3 f(x) \, dx - \int_0^1 f(x) \, dx \\ &= 5 - 2 \\ &= 3 \end{aligned}\]
Jadi, nilai \(\displaystyle\int_1^3 f(x) \, dx = 3\).

Pembahasan:

Gunakan teknik substitusi:

Misalkan: \[u = x^2 + 1 \implies du = 2x \, dx \implies x \, dx = \dfrac{1}{2} \, du\]

Batas integrasi:

  • Untuk \(x = 0 \implies u = 0^2 + 1 = 1\)
  • Untuk \(x = 1 \implies u = 1^2 + 1 = 2\)

Substitusikan ke integral:

\[\begin{aligned} \int_0^1 x \cdot f(x^2 + 1) \, dx &= \int_1^2 f(u) \cdot \dfrac{1}{2} \, du \\ &= \dfrac{1}{2} \int_1^2 f(u) \, du \\ &= \dfrac{1}{2} (4) \\ &= 2 \end{aligned}\]
Jadi, nilai dari \(\displaystyle\int_0^1 x \cdot f(x^2 + 1) \, dx = 2\).
