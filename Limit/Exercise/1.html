\[\lim_{x\to3}\dfrac{3-\sqrt{2x+3}}{x-3}=\dots\]

Dunia Matematika Indonesia
Pembahasan:

Cara 1: Mengalikan dengan Bentuk Sekawan

\[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{x\to3}\dfrac{3-\sqrt{2x+3}}{x-3}&=\lim_{x\to3}\dfrac{3-\sqrt{2x+3}}{x-3}\cdot\dfrac{3+\sqrt{2x+3}}{3+\sqrt{2x+3}}\\[3.7pt] &=\lim_{x\to3}\dfrac{9-(2x+3)}{(x-3)\left(3+\sqrt{2x+3}\right)}\\[3.7pt] &=\lim_{x\to3}\dfrac{6-2x}{(x-3)\left(3+\sqrt{2x+3}\right)}\\[3.7pt] &=\lim_{x\to3}\dfrac{-2(x-3)}{(x-3)\left(3+\sqrt{2x+3}\right)}\\[3.7pt] &=\lim_{x\to3}\dfrac{-2}{3+\sqrt{2x+3}}\\[3.7pt] &=\dfrac{-2}{3+\sqrt{2(3)+3}}\\[3.7pt] &=\dfrac{-2}{3+3}\\[3.7pt] &=-\dfrac{2}{6} = -\dfrac{1}{3} \end{aligned} \]

Cara 2: Aturan L'Hôpital

Karena substitusi langsung menghasilkan bentuk tak tentu \(\frac{0}{0}\), turunkan pembilang dan penyebut terhadap \(x\):

\[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{x\to3}\dfrac{3-\sqrt{2x+3}}{x-3}&=\lim_{x\to3}\dfrac{0-\dfrac{2}{2\sqrt{2x+3}}}{1}\\[3.7pt] &=\lim_{x\to3}\left(-\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2x+3}}\right)\\[3.7pt] &=-\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2(3)+3}}\\[3.7pt] &=-\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{9}} = -\dfrac{1}{3} \end{aligned} \]
Jadi, \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to3}\dfrac{3-\sqrt{2x+3}}{x-3}=-\dfrac{1}{3}\).

Jadi, \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to3}\dfrac{\left(\sqrt{x-1}-\sqrt{5-x}\right)\left(\sqrt{4x-3}-1\right)}{x^2-9}=\dfrac{1}{6}\sqrt{2}\).

Pembahasan:

Faktorkan penyebut \(x^2-x-2 = (x-2)(x+1)\) lalu samakan penyebutnya:

\[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{x\to2}\left(\dfrac{6}{x^2-x-2}-\dfrac{2}{x-2}\right)&=\lim_{x\to2}\left(\dfrac{6}{(x-2)(x+1)}-\dfrac{2}{x-2}\right)\\[3.7pt] &=\lim_{x\to2}\dfrac{6-2(x+1)}{(x-2)(x+1)}\\[3.7pt] &=\lim_{x\to2}\dfrac{6-2x-2}{(x-2)(x+1)}\\[3.7pt] &=\lim_{x\to2}\dfrac{-2x+4}{(x-2)(x+1)}\\[3.7pt] &=\lim_{x\to2}\dfrac{-2(x-2)}{(x-2)(x+1)}\\[3.7pt] &=\lim_{x\to2}\dfrac{-2}{x+1}\\[3.7pt] &=\dfrac{-2}{2+1} = -\dfrac{2}{3} \end{aligned} \]
Jadi, \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to2}\left(\dfrac{6}{x^2-x-2}-\dfrac{2}{x-2}\right)=-\dfrac{2}{3}\).

\[\lim_{x\to-2}\dfrac{x^3-x^2-2x+8}{x^3+8}=\dots\]

Ganesha Operation
Pembahasan:

Cara 1: Pemfaktoran

Faktorkan pembilang dan penyebut dengan membagi \((x+2)\):

\[ \begin{aligned} x^3-x^2-2x+8 &= (x+2)(x^2-3x+4)\\[3.7pt] x^3+8 &= (x+2)(x^2-2x+4) \end{aligned} \] Sehingga: \[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{x\to-2}\dfrac{x^3-x^2-2x+8}{x^3+8}&=\lim_{x\to-2}\dfrac{(x+2)\left(x^2-3x+4\right)}{(x+2)\left(x^2-2x+4\right)}\\[3.7pt] &=\lim_{x\to-2}\dfrac{x^2-3x+4}{x^2-2x+4}\\[3.7pt] &=\dfrac{(-2)^2-3(-2)+4}{(-2)^2-2(-2)+4}\\[3.7pt] &=\dfrac{4+6+4}{4+4+4}\\[3.7pt] &=\dfrac{14}{12} = \dfrac{7}{6} \end{aligned} \]

Cara 2: Aturan L'Hôpital

\[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{x\to-2}\dfrac{x^3-x^2-2x+8}{x^3+8}&=\lim_{x\to-2}\dfrac{3x^2-2x-2}{3x^2}\\[3.7pt] &=\dfrac{3(-2)^2-2(-2)-2}{3(-2)^2}\\[3.7pt] &=\dfrac{12+4-2}{12}\\[3.7pt] &=\dfrac{14}{12} = \dfrac{7}{6} \end{aligned} \]
Jadi, \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to-2}\dfrac{x^3-x^2-2x+8}{x^3+8}=\dfrac{7}{6}\).

Tentukan nilai dari: \[\lim_{x\to5}\dfrac{x^2-25}{\sqrt{x^2+24}-7}\]

Ganesha Operation
Pembahasan:

Cara 1: Mengalikan dengan Sekawan

\[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{x\to5}\dfrac{x^2-25}{\sqrt{x^2+24}-7}&=\lim_{x\to5}\dfrac{x^2-25}{\sqrt{x^2+24}-7}\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt{x^2+24}+7}{\sqrt{x^2+24}+7}\\[3.7pt] &=\lim_{x\to5}\dfrac{\left(x^2-25\right)\left(\sqrt{x^2+24}+7\right)}{(x^2+24)-49}\\[3.7pt] &=\lim_{x\to5}\dfrac{\left(x^2-25\right)\left(\sqrt{x^2+24}+7\right)}{x^2-25}\\[3.7pt] &=\lim_{x\to5}\left(\sqrt{x^2+24}+7\right)\\[3.7pt] &=\sqrt{5^2+24}+7\\[3.7pt] &=\sqrt{49}+7 = 7+7 = 14 \end{aligned} \]

Cara 2: Aturan L'Hôpital

\[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{x\to5}\dfrac{x^2-25}{\sqrt{x^2+24}-7}&=\lim_{x\to5}\dfrac{2x}{\dfrac{2x}{2\sqrt{x^2+24}}}\\[3.7pt] &=\lim_{x\to5}2\sqrt{x^2+24}\\[3.7pt] &=2\sqrt{5^2+24}\\[3.7pt] &=2\sqrt{49} = 2(7) = 14 \end{aligned} \]
Jadi, \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to5}\dfrac{x^2-25}{\sqrt{x^2+24}-7}=14\).

Tentukan nilai dari: \[\lim_{x\to2}\dfrac{x^3+2x^2-4x-8}{x^3-8}\]

Ganesha Operation
Pembahasan:

Cara 1: Pemfaktoran

Faktorkan pembilang dan penyebut:

\[ \begin{aligned} x^3+2x^2-4x-8 &= x^2(x+2)-4(x+2) = (x^2-4)(x+2) = (x-2)(x+2)^2 = (x-2)(x^2+4x+4)\\[3.7pt] x^3-8 &= (x-2)(x^2+2x+4) \end{aligned} \] Maka: \[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{x\to2}\dfrac{x^3+2x^2-4x-8}{x^3-8}&=\lim_{x\to2}\dfrac{(x-2)\left(x^2+4x+4\right)}{(x-2)\left(x^2+2x+4\right)}\\[3.7pt] &=\dfrac{2^2+4(2)+4}{2^2+2(2)+4}\\[3.7pt] &=\dfrac{4+8+4}{4+4+4}\\[3.7pt] &=\dfrac{16}{12} = \dfrac{4}{3} \end{aligned} \]

Cara 2: Aturan L'Hôpital

\[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{x\to2}\dfrac{x^3+2x^2-4x-8}{x^3-8}&=\lim_{x\to2}\dfrac{3x^2+4x-4}{3x^2}\\[3.7pt] &=\dfrac{3(2)^2+4(2)-4}{3(2)^2}\\[3.7pt] &=\dfrac{12+8-4}{12}\\[3.7pt] &=\dfrac{16}{12} = \dfrac{4}{3} \end{aligned} \]
Jadi, \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to2}\dfrac{x^3+2x^2-4x-8}{x^3-8}=\dfrac{4}{3}\).

Jika \(f(x)=\cos\left(\dfrac{1}{2}x\right)\), maka nilai dari \[\lim_{h\to0}\dfrac{f\left(x+\dfrac{h}{2}\right)-2f(x)+f\left(x-\dfrac{h}{2}\right)}{h^2}=\dots\]

  1. \(\dfrac{1}{2}\sin 2x\)
  2. \(-\dfrac{1}{16}\cos\dfrac{1}{2}x\)
  3. \(-\dfrac{1}{16}\sin\dfrac{1}{2}x\)
  4. \(\dfrac{1}{16}\cos 2x\)
  5. \(-\dfrac{1}{16}\sin 2x\)
Pembahasan:

Cara 1: Penjabaran Trigonometri

\[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{h\to0}\dfrac{f\left(x+\dfrac{h}{2}\right)-2f(x)+f\left(x-\dfrac{h}{2}\right)}{h^2}&=\lim_{h\to0}\dfrac{\cos\left(\dfrac{1}{2}x+\dfrac{h}{4}\right)-2\cos\left(\dfrac{1}{2}x\right)+\cos\left(\dfrac{1}{2}x-\dfrac{h}{4}\right)}{h^2} \end{aligned} \] Gunakan identitas penjumlahan kosinus \(\cos(A+B)+\cos(A-B)=2\cos A\cos B\): \[ \begin{aligned} &=\lim_{h\to0}\dfrac{2\cos\left(\dfrac{1}{2}x\right)\cos\left(\dfrac{h}{4}\right)-2\cos\left(\dfrac{1}{2}x\right)}{h^2}\\[3.7pt] &=\lim_{h\to0}\dfrac{2\cos\left(\dfrac{1}{2}x\right)\left(\cos\left(\dfrac{h}{4}\right)-1\right)}{h^2}\\[3.7pt] &=\lim_{h\to0}\dfrac{2\cos\left(\dfrac{1}{2}x\right)\left(-2\sin^2\left(\dfrac{h}{8}\right)\right)}{h^2}\\[3.7pt] &=\lim_{h\to0}\left(-4\cos\left(\dfrac{1}{2}x\right)\right)\cdot\dfrac{\sin\left(\dfrac{h}{8}\right)}{h}\cdot\dfrac{\sin\left(\dfrac{h}{8}\right)}{h}\\[3.7pt] &=-4\cos\left(\dfrac{1}{2}x\right)\cdot\dfrac{1}{8}\cdot\dfrac{1}{8} = -\dfrac{1}{16}\cos\left(\dfrac{1}{2}x\right) \end{aligned} \]

Cara 2: Konsep Turunan Kedua

Limit di atas merupakan bentuk limit beda hingga pusat untuk turunan kedua \(f''(x)\):

\[ \begin{aligned} f'(x) &= -\dfrac{1}{2}\sin\left(\dfrac{1}{2}x\right)\\[3.7pt] f''(x) &= -\dfrac{1}{4}\cos\left(\dfrac{1}{2}x\right) \end{aligned} \] Dengan aturan L'Hôpital terhadap variabel \(h\): \[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{h\to0}\dfrac{f\left(x+\dfrac{h}{2}\right)-2f(x)+f\left(x-\dfrac{h}{2}\right)}{h^2}&=\lim_{h\to0}\dfrac{\frac{1}{2}f'\left(x+\dfrac{h}{2}\right)-\frac{1}{2}f'\left(x-\dfrac{h}{2}\right)}{2h}\\[3.7pt] &=\lim_{h\to0}\dfrac{\frac{1}{4}f''\left(x+\dfrac{h}{2}\right)+\frac{1}{4}f''\left(x-\dfrac{h}{2}\right)}{2}\\[3.7pt] &=\dfrac{\frac{1}{2}f''(x)}{2} = \dfrac{1}{4}f''(x)\\[3.7pt] &=\dfrac{1}{4}\left(-\dfrac{1}{4}\cos\left(\dfrac{1}{2}x\right)\right) = -\dfrac{1}{16}\cos\left(\dfrac{1}{2}x\right) \end{aligned} \]
Jadi, hasilnya adalah \(-\dfrac{1}{16}\cos\left(\dfrac{1}{2}x\right)\).

Diketahui \(f(x) = x^2 + 2x\). Nilai dari \[\lim_{h\to0}\dfrac{f(x-2h)-f(x)}{4h}=\dots\]

Matematika Zone Id
Pembahasan:

Substitusikan \(f(x-2h)\) ke dalam bentuk limit:

\[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{h\to0}\dfrac{f(x-2h)-f(x)}{4h}&=\lim_{h\to0}\dfrac{\left[(x-2h)^2+2(x-2h)\right]-\left[x^2+2x\right]}{4h}\\[3.7pt] &=\lim_{h\to0}\dfrac{\left(x^2-4xh+4h^2+2x-4h\right)-\left(x^2+2x\right)}{4h}\\[3.7pt] &=\lim_{h\to0}\dfrac{-4xh+4h^2-4h}{4h}\\[3.7pt] &=\lim_{h\to0}(-x+h-1)\\[3.7pt] &=-x+0-1 = -x-1 \end{aligned} \]

Alternatif Turunan:

\[ \lim_{h\to0}\dfrac{f(x-2h)-f(x)}{4h} = \lim_{h\to0}\dfrac{f(x+(-2h))-f(x)}{-2h}\cdot\left(-\dfrac{1}{2}\right) = -\dfrac{1}{2}f'(x) = -\dfrac{1}{2}(2x+2) = -x-1 \]
Jadi, \(\displaystyle\lim_{h\to0}\dfrac{f(x-2h)-f(x)}{4h}=-x-1\).

Jika \(f(x)=\dfrac{x-\sqrt{x}}{x+\sqrt{x}}\), maka nilai \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to0}f(x)=\dots\)

  1. \(0\)
  2. \(-\dfrac{1}{2}\)
  3. \(-1\)
  4. \(-2\)
  5. \(\infty\)
Pembahasan:

Cara 1: Membagi dengan \(\sqrt{x}\)

Faktorkan \(\sqrt{x}\) dari pembilang dan penyebut (untuk \(x > 0\)):

\[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{x-\sqrt{x}}{x+\sqrt{x}}&=\lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{\sqrt{x}(\sqrt{x}-1)}{\sqrt{x}(\sqrt{x}+1)}\\[3.7pt] &=\lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{\sqrt{x}-1}{\sqrt{x}+1}\\[3.7pt] &=\dfrac{\sqrt{0}-1}{\sqrt{0}+1}\\[3.7pt] &=\dfrac{-1}{1} = -1 \end{aligned} \]

Cara 2: Aturan L'Hôpital

\[ \begin{aligned} \lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{x-\sqrt{x}}{x+\sqrt{x}}&=\lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{1-\dfrac{1}{2\sqrt{x}}}{1+\dfrac{1}{2\sqrt{x}}}\cdot\dfrac{2\sqrt{x}}{2\sqrt{x}}\\[3.7pt] &=\lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{2\sqrt{x}-1}{2\sqrt{x}+1}\\[3.7pt] &=\dfrac{2(0)-1}{2(0)+1} = -1 \end{aligned} \]
Jadi, \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to0}f(x)=-1\).
