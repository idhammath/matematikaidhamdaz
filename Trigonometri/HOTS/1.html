Misalkan

$$\sin\left(\sin(5x-4y)\cdot\sin4(x-y)\right)=a,$$

dan

$$\cos\left(\cos(5x-4y)\cdot\cos4(x-y)\right)=b$$

dengan $0\lt y\lt x\lt \dfrac{1}{20}\pi$. Jika dinyatakan dalam $a$ dan $b$ maka $\cos(\cos x) =$ ....

Pembahasan:
$$\begin{aligned} \sin\left(\sin(5x-4y)\cdot\sin4(x-y)\right)&=a\\ \sin(5x-4y)\cdot\sin(4x-4y)&=\sin^{-1}a&{\color{red}(1)} \end{aligned}$$ $$\begin{aligned} \cos\left(\cos(5x-4y)\cdot\cos4(x-y)\right)&=b\\ \cos(5x-4y)\cdot\cos(4x-4y)&=\cos^{-1}b&{\color{red}(2)} \end{aligned}$$

(1) + (2)

$$\begin{array}{rcl} \sin(5x-4y)\cdot\sin(4x-4y)&=&\sin^{-1}a\\ \cos(5x-4y)\cdot\cos(4x-4y)&=&\cos^{-1}b\quad{\color{red}+}\\ \hline \cos(5x-4y)\cdot\cos(4x-4y)+\sin(5x-4y)\cdot\sin(4x-4y)&=&\sin^{-1}a+\cos^{-1}b \end{array}$$ $$\begin{aligned} \cos\left((5x-4y)-(4x-4y)\right)&=\sin^{-1}a+\cos^{-1}b\\ \cos x&=\sin^{-1}a+\cos^{-1}b\\ \cos(\cos x)&=\cos\left(\sin^{-1}a+\cos^{-1}b\right)\\ &=\cos\left(\sin^{-1}a\right)\cdot\cos\left(\cos^{-1}b\right)-\sin\left(\sin^{-1}a\right)\cdot\sin\left(\cos^{-1}b\right)\\ &=\boxed{\boxed{b\sqrt{1-a^2}-a\sqrt{1-b^2}}} \end{aligned}$$
Jadi, $\cos(\cos x)=b\sqrt{1-a^2}-a\sqrt{1-b^2}$.

Pembahasan:
$$\begin{aligned} \left(\sin\left(15^\circ\right)+\sin\left(75^\circ\right)\right)^2&=\sin^2\left(15^\circ\right)+\sin^2\left(75^\circ\right)+2\sin\left(15^\circ\right)\sin\left(75^\circ\right)\\ &=\sin^2\left(15^\circ\right)+\cos^2\left(15^\circ\right)+2\sin\left(15^\circ\right)\cos\left(15^\circ\right)\\ &=1+\sin\left(30^\circ\right)\\ &=1+\dfrac12\\ &=\boxed{\boxed{\dfrac32}} \end{aligned}$$
Jadi, $\left(\sin\left(15^\circ\right)+\sin\left(75^\circ\right)\right)^2=\dfrac32$. (JAWAB: B)

Jika $\sin^{-1}x+\sin^{-1}y+\sin^{-1}z=\dfrac{3\pi}{2}$, maka nilai dari $x^{2023}+y^{2023}+z^{2023}+\dfrac{27}{x^{2023}+y^{2023}+z^{2023}}$ adalah ....

  1. 0
  2. 3
  3. 6
  4. 9
  5. 12
KPATN 2024 Jenjang Guru
Pembahasan:

Perhatikan bahwa $-\dfrac{\pi}{2}\leq\sin^{-1}\alpha\leq\dfrac{\pi}{2}$, sehingga $-\dfrac{3\pi}{2}\leq\sin^{-1}x+\sin^{-1}y+\sin^{-1}z\leq\dfrac{3\pi}{2}$. Karena $\sin^{-1}x+\sin^{-1}y+\sin^{-1}z=\dfrac{3\pi}{2}$, maka $\sin^{-1}x=\sin^{-1}y=\sin^{-1}z=\dfrac{\pi}{2}$. Didapat

$x=y=z=\sin\dfrac{\pi}{2}=1$

$$\begin{aligned} x^{2023}+y^{2023}+z^{2023}+\dfrac{27}{x^{2023}+y^{2023}+z^{2023}}&=1^{2023}+1^{2023}+1^{2023}+\dfrac{27}{1^{2023}+1^{2023}+1^{2023}}\\[4pt] &=3+\dfrac{27}{3}\\[4pt] &=3+9\\ &=\boxed{\boxed{12}} \end{aligned}$$
Jadi, nilai dari $x^{2023}+y^{2023}+z^{2023}+\dfrac{27}{x^{2023}+y^{2023}+z^{2023}}$ adalah 12. (JAWAB: E)

Jika $\sqrt{\sin^4x+4\cos^2x}-\sqrt{\cos^4x+4\sin^2x}=\dfrac13$, maka nilai $6\cos 2x$ adalah ....

Evaluasi GMOM Februari 2024 tingkat SMA
Pembahasan:
$$\begin{aligned} \sqrt{\sin^4x+4\cos^2x}-\sqrt{\cos^4x+4\sin^2x}&=\dfrac13\\[4pt] \sqrt{\sin^4x+4\left(1-\sin^2x\right)}-\sqrt{\cos^4x+4\left(1-\cos^2x\right)}&=\dfrac13\\[4pt] \sqrt{\sin^4x-4\sin^2x+4}-\sqrt{\cos^4x-4\cos^2x+4}&=\dfrac13\\[4pt] \sqrt{\left(\sin^2x-2\right)^2}-\sqrt{\left(\cos^2x-2\right)^2}&=\dfrac13\\[4pt] \left|\sin^2x-2\right|-\left|\cos^2x-2\right|&=\dfrac13 \end{aligned}$$

Perhatikan bahwa $\sin^2 x \le 1$ dan $\cos^2 x \le 1$, maka $\sin^2 x - 2$ dan $\cos^2 x - 2$ bernilai negatif, sehingga

$$\begin{aligned} \left|\sin^2x-2\right|-\left|\cos^2x-2\right|&=\dfrac13\\[4pt] 2-\sin^2x-\left(2-\cos^2x\right)&=\dfrac13\\[4pt] 2-\sin^2x-2+\cos^2x&=\dfrac13\\[4pt] \cos^2x-\sin^2x&=\dfrac13\\[4pt] \cos2x&=\dfrac13\\ 6\cos2x&=\boxed{\boxed{2}} \end{aligned}$$
Jadi, nilai $6\cos 2x$ adalah 2.

Jika $\sin A=\sqrt{2pq}$ dan $\tan A=\dfrac{\sqrt{2pq}}{p-q}$, maka $p^2 + q^2 =$ ....

Evaluasi GMOM September 2024
Pembahasan:
$$\begin{aligned} \tan A&=\dfrac{\sqrt{2pq}}{p-q}\\[4pt] \dfrac{\sin A}{\cos A}&=\dfrac{\sqrt{2pq}}{p-q}\\[4pt] \dfrac{\sqrt{2pq}}{\cos A}&=\dfrac{\sqrt{2pq}}{p-q}\\[4pt] \cos A&=p-q \end{aligned}$$ $$\begin{aligned} \sin^2A+\cos^2A&=1\\ \left(\sqrt{2pq}\right)^2+(p-q)^2&=1\\ 2pq+p^2-2pq+q^2&=1\\ p^2+q^2&=\boxed{\boxed{1}} \end{aligned}$$
Jadi, $p^2 + q^2 = 1$.

Nilai dari

$$\cos(56^\circ)\cdot\cos(2\cdot56^\circ)\cdot\cos(2^2\cdot56^\circ)\cdots\cos(2^{23}\cdot56^\circ)$$

adalah ....

  1. $2^{-24}$
  2. $2^{-23}$
  3. $2^{24}$
  4. $2^{23}$
  5. $2^{-28}$
Penyisihan LMNas UGM ke-28
Pembahasan:
$$\begin{aligned} \sin2a&=2\sin a\cos a\\ \cos a&=\dfrac{\sin 2a}{2\sin a} \end{aligned}$$

Misalkan $P=\cos(56^\circ)\cdot\cos(2\cdot56^\circ)\cdot\cos(2^2\cdot56^\circ)\cdots\cos(2^{23}\cdot56^\circ)$. Maka

$$\begin{aligned} P&=\dfrac{\sin\left(2\cdot56^\circ\right)}{2\sin\left(56^\circ\right)}\cdot\dfrac{\sin\left(2^2\cdot56^\circ\right)}{2\sin\left(2\cdot56^\circ\right)}\cdot\dfrac{\sin\left(2^3\cdot56^\circ\right)}{2\sin\left(2^2\cdot56^\circ\right)}\cdots\dfrac{\sin\left(2^{24}\cdot56^\circ\right)}{2\sin\left(2^{23}\cdot56^\circ\right)}\\[4pt] &=\dfrac{\sin\left(2^{24}\cdot56^\circ\right)}{2^{24}\sin\left(56^\circ\right)} \end{aligned}$$

Kita buktikan bahwa $2^{24}\cdot56^\circ = 56^\circ + k\cdot360^\circ$, atau $2^{24}\cdot56 \equiv 56 \pmod{360}$.

$$\begin{aligned} 2^{24}\cdot56\bmod360&\equiv8\left(2^{24}\cdot7\bmod45\right)\bmod360\\ &\equiv8\left(7\bmod45\right)\bmod360&\ {\color{red}\phi(45)=24}\\ &\equiv56\bmod360 \end{aligned}$$ $$\begin{aligned} P=\dfrac{\sin\left(2^{24}\cdot56^\circ\right)}{2^{24}\sin\left(56^\circ\right)}&=\dfrac{\sin\left(56^\circ\right)}{2^{24}\sin\left(56^\circ\right)}\\[4pt] &=\dfrac{1}{2^{24}}\\ &=\boxed{\boxed{2^{-24}}} \end{aligned}$$
Jadi, $\cos(56^\circ)\cdot\cos(2\cdot56^\circ)\cdot\cos(2^2\cdot56^\circ)\cdots\cos(2^{23}\cdot56^\circ) = 2^{-24}$. (JAWAB: A)

Jika sudut lancip $\theta$ memenuhi persamaan $\sin\theta=\dfrac{3}{5}$, manakah dari pilihan berikut ini yang benar?

  1. $0^\circ < \theta < 30^\circ$
  2. $30^\circ < \theta < 45^\circ$
  3. $45^\circ < \theta < 60^\circ$
  4. $60^\circ < \theta < 75^\circ$
  5. $75^\circ < \theta < 90^\circ$
Penyisihan OGM 10 Tingkat SMA
Pembahasan:

Perhatikan bahwa jika $0^\circ < \alpha < \theta < \beta < 90^\circ$, maka $0 < \sin \alpha < \sin \theta < \sin \beta < 1$.

Perhatikan tabel nilai sin berikut ini:

$\theta$ $0^\circ$ $30^\circ$ $45^\circ$ $60^\circ$ $90^\circ$
$\sin\theta$ $0$ $\dfrac12=0{,}5$ $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}=0{,}7$ $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}=0{,}86$ $1$

Karena $\sin\theta=\dfrac35=0{,}6$, maka $\theta$ berada di antara $30^\circ$ dan $45^\circ$.

Jadi, $30^\circ < \theta < 45^\circ$. (JAWAB: B)
